🧠 Best Deal Analysis Framework (Updated — 2026-06-02)

Pre-Filter Layer: 3-Metric Gateway

  1. Gross Yield Quick Kill: ≥5% BTL, ≥10% HMO, ≥15% SA. If below → reject instantly. Prevents analysis paralysis. DealSheetAI
  2. Price-per-Sq-Ft Cross-Check: Calculate £/sq ft and compare to area average. Above average = premium location (lower yield expected). Below average = potential undervalue. Use EPC register for exact sqm. Farrell Heyworth
  3. Days-on-Market Signal: 20-40d = high demand, 40-70d = balanced, 70+ = potential pricing issue or weak demand. Falling DOM + rising prices = strong market signal. Rising DOM + falling prices = weakening market. Farrell Heyworth

Stage 1 — Deal Identification & Market Context

  1. Distress signals first: Probate, sitting tenant, "requires modernisation", 90+ days, multiple reductions, "cash buyers preferred"
  2. Reject signals: "Recently refurbished" (no value-add — this kills BRRR/Flip), lease <80yrs, unmortgageable construction
  3. Cross-validate price: Check asking vs Land Registry vs Rightmove sold vs £/sq ft area average. Use 3+ data sources minimum.
  4. Supply/demand check: Search Rightmove lettings with 'let agreed' ticked. 35%+ = strong tenant demand. Below suggests weak market. Jamie York

Stage 2 — True Cost Waterfall

Total Investment = Purchase + SDLT (3% surcharge) + Legal (£1.5k) + Survey (£500) + Broker (£500) + Refurb + Contingency (15-20%) + Holding Costs (£6-10k standard)

Annual Costs = Management (12%) + Maintenance (10% preventive / 15% reactive) + Voids (12.5% WM min) + Insurance (£35/mo) + Compliance (£25/mo) + MTD (£10/mo) + Bad-Debt Contingency (22% of rent at 3-yr tenure)

Stage 3 — Four-Layer Profitability Display

Every Deal Card MUST show all four:
1. Gross Yield % = (Annual Rent ÷ Purchase Price) × 100 — "Quick filter, dangerously misleading alone"
2. Net Yield % = (Annual Net Income ÷ Total Investment Cost) × 100 — "Asset profitability"
3. Cash-on-Cash ROI % = (Annual Net Cashflow ÷ Total Cash Invested) × 100 — "Your cash efficiency. Target 8-12%."
4. Total Return % = Net Yield + Annualised Capital Growth — "Combined income + appreciation. Target 15-20%."

Stage 4 — Strategy Fit & Risk

  1. Professional team first: Solicitor, BRRR-aware surveyor, specialist broker before detailed analysis. DealSheetAI
  2. Match strategy to person: Finances, time, risk tolerance, experience — not what's trending. Residential Estates
  3. Yield benchmarking: London 3-4%, NW 6-8%+, HMOs 9-11%, SA 12-15%. Commercial 8%+. Know your target range. MaddisonV + August + DealSheetAI
  4. 3-exit scenarios: Primary (BTL), secondary (sell), tertiary (HMO). Stress test each.

📈 Financial Models

Total Return Framework (NEW — 2026-06-02)

Total Return % = Net Yield + Annualised Capital Growth %
Leveraged Total Return = Net Yield + (Capital Growth × Leverage Factor)
Leverage Factor = Property Value ÷ Cash Invested (4× at 75% LTV)

Example A: London flat: 4% net yield + 5% growth = 9% total return
Example B: Northern BTL: 8% net yield + 0% growth = 8% total return
Insight: The "yield-growth seesaw" means higher-yield areas can underperform if capital growth is zero. Always assess both.

Price-per-Sq-Ft Cross-Validation (NEW — 2026-06-02)

£/sq ft = Purchase Price ÷ Gross Internal Area (sq ft)
Comparison target: Area average £/sq ft from 3+ comparable sold properties
Below area average: Potential undervalue or condition issues → investigate further
Above area average: Premium location or overpriced → lower yield expected
Source: EPC register for exact sqm (convert: 1 sqm = 10.764 sq ft)

⚠️ Common Mistakes (New for 2026-06-02)

  • Not calculating Total Return: Comparing properties on yield alone misses the capital growth component. A 4% yield + 5% growth property can outperform an 8% yield + 0% growth property over 5 years. Always model both. MaddisonV + Farrell Heyworth (convergent)
  • Ignoring £/sq ft as a valuation tool: Price alone doesn't tell you if a property is overvalued. A £200k terrace may be overpriced at £380/sq ft when the area averages £280/sq ft. Use this as a cross-check against comparable evidence. Farrell Heyworth
  • Reading market headlines instead of local signals: National "flat" UK data hides strong regional growth. Local expertise beats national averages. Combine price growth + DOM + supply/demand for true local signal. Farrell Heyworth
  • Confusing gross yield with safe return: 3 independent sources confirm: a 6.33% gross can deliver 2.1% cash-on-cash. A 5.2% gross is "a silent signal of a portfolio losing its edge" in 2026. Gross yield alone is not investment analysis. MaddisonV, August App, DealSheetAI
  • Chasing strategy trends instead of personal fit: HMO, SA, R2R — each has radically different management requirements. "Best" strategy doesn't exist — only what fits your finances, time, risk tolerance, and experience. Residential Estates, PIN/Simon Zutshi
  • Skipping professional education before capital deployment: "Cost of education is almost always less than cost of mistakes." Investors who learn early move faster and make better decisions. Residential Estates
  • Assuming "this looks like a good deal" is sufficient: Market data + calculated numbers + stress testing are non-negotiable. Agent projections and optimistic rental assumptions are the root cause of most deal failures. Residential Estates, PIN

Upgrade A — Total Return Framework (New Stage 3 Metric, Layer 4):
Add a fourth profitability layer alongside Gross Yield, Net Yield, and Cash-on-Cash ROI — Total Return % (Net Yield + Annualised Capital Growth). This is the single most important missing metric in our current framework.

  • Formula: Total Return % = Net Yield + Annualised Capital Growth %. For leveraged properties: Net Yield + (Capital Growth × Leverage Factor where Leverage Factor = Property Value ÷ Cash Invested).
  • Why it's needed: The MaddisonV guide explicitly calls out that 4% London yield + 5% capital growth = 9% total return, which can outperform 8% Northern yield + 0% growth = 8% total return over a 5-year hold. Our current framework has no metric that captures this dynamic — meaning we systematically undervalue growth-area deals and overvalue stagnant-area deals with high yields.
  • Rule: Target 15-20% combined total return for WM BTL. Below 12% → flag as underperforming asset.
  • Impact: Properties that previously scored high on yield alone will be compared against growth-adjusted returns. This may shift recommendations toward Birmingham HS2 corridor, Wolverhampton regeneration zones, and Dudley town centre (emerging growth) over higher-yield but stagnant postcodes.

Upgrade B — Price-per-Sq-Ft Cross-Validation (Stage 0 / Stage 2):
Add £/sq ft as a mandatory cross-validation metric in both the Triage (Stage 0) and True Market Value (Stage 2) stages.

  • Farrell Heyworth: "Price per sq ft is the hidden indicator that allows accurate comparison of properties regardless of size." Higher £/sq ft → premium locations. Lower £/sq ft → potential undervalued opportunities. Sudden increases → rising local demand.
  • Integration: In Stage 0 triage, add a -1 penalty if £/sq ft is significantly above area average (overpriced risk). In Stage 2, use £/sq ft alongside comparable evidence as a cross-check — significant deviation warrants investigation.
  • Use EPC register for exact GIA square metres. Convert: 1 sqm = 10.764 sq ft.

Upgrade C — Data Combination Signals (Stage 3 — Area Intelligence):
Add a framework for combining market data indicators into actionable signals, replacing single-metric analysis.

  • Farrell Heyworth: "No single metric tells the full story. Experts combine multiple data points to form a complete view." Specific signal combinations include: Price growth + Falling DOM = strong demand. Stable prices + Rising supply = potential slowdown. High yields + Low growth = income investment. Falling DOM + Falling prices = distress signal.
  • Integration: Add a "Market Signal" row to every Deal Card's area intelligence section. Use the combination table to label the signal as STRONG, CAUTION, or DISTRESS.
  • This replaces "here are some numbers" with "here is what the data is telling us about this market."

All 10 sources agreed on: (1) gross yield is insufficient without net yield and CoC, (2) compliance costs and regulatory burden are structurally increasing, (3) market data must be localised, not national, and (4) professional team/education before capital deployment is now non-negotiable.
