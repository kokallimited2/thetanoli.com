⚠️ Common Mistakes — New Additions

2026-05-21
  • Treating HMO as a "bigger rent" version of standard BTL in BRRR analysis — HMO BRRRR has 5 separate workstream gates (planning, licensing, building regs, compliance, layout) that standard BTL doesn't. Treating them as interchangeable is the #1 HMO BRRR failure cause. (Source: HMO Architects)
  • Assuming only professional management is an "extra cost" — Multiple 2026 sources confirm the 10% management fee is net-positive: firms fill voids 50% faster, negotiate better rents, and handle compliance. Self-management reliably costs MORE in hidden costs (vacancy, pricing errors, legal risk, time opportunity). (Sources: 10 Acre, Link Property)
  • Using a flat maintenance % without distinguishing preventive vs reactive — A 10% reserve for preventive maintenance (inspections, servicing) is adequate. A 10% reserve for reactive maintenance (emergency callouts, urgent replacement) will be exhausted. Budget 12-15% for reactive, 8-10% for preventive. (Source: Link Property)
  • Built entirely on best-case assumptions without stress testing named as "blind confidence" — "Blind confidence is more dangerous than uncertainty." If every input in the analysis is optimistic (best rent, lowest void, cheapest refurb, no rate rises), flag it explicitly as "Blind Confidence — Full stress testing required." (Source: Simon Zutshi / PIN)
  • Not recognising that the gross-to-net yield gap is structural, not marginal — 4 independent sources (Residential Landlord, Property Filter, DealSheetAI, 10acre) converge on 8% gross → 3-4% net. This is now market consensus, not a conservative assumption. Our 50% haircut for WM screening is validated.
