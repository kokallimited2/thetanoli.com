  • ☐ Grade II Listed Building — Netherton Arts Centre (£500k, Grade II). Severe constraints.

  • ☐ Green Belt — Walsall appeals being won but only with 50% affordable via grey belt rules + legal challenges possible (Walsall Council challenged Longwood Lane decision)
  • ☐ Flood Zone 3 — Check council flood maps before ANY purchase
  • ☐ Article 4 Direction — Birmingham HMO A4 since June 2020; Wolverhampton since Sept 2017
  • ☐ Japanese Knotweed — Verify treatment history. Untreated = -2.0 score penalty.
  • ☐ Coal Mining (CON29M) — MANDATORY for ALL DY/WV postcodes. Budget £5k-£20k+ for investigation.
  • ☐ Birmingham land premiums — Expect 50-177% premium at auction (Stone Rd Edgbaston: £100k→£277k = 177%). Budget 2-3x guide.

  • ☐ 10% Biodiversity Net Gain — Mandatory for ALL planning permissions. Budget £5k-£20k depending on site.
  • ☐ Bond Wolfe ground rent investments — Some lots have peppercorn ground rents with no review mechanism. Check legal pack carefully.
