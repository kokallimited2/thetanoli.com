⚠️ Planning Risk Checklist

🔴 High Risk Factors

  • Access and highways — #1 refusal reason in West Midlands. Ketley Quarry stalled 2 years on road layout. Always commission a transport statement early.
  • Flood risk — Land near canals (Stourbridge Canal, Dudley Canal, Birmingham Canal system) often in Flood Zone 2/3. Check EA flood maps first.
  • Green belt — Significant areas of West Midlands are Green Belt (especially Sutton Coldfield, south Birmingham, parts of Dudley/Wyre Forest). Check local plan policy.
  • Conservation area — Selly Park, Edgbaston, parts of Dudley town centre are in conservation areas. Article 4 directions restrict permitted development.
  • Listed building constraints — Great Barr Hall example shows how Grade II listing can stall schemes for years. Always instruct heritage assessment.

🟡 Medium Risk Factors

  • Neighbour objections — Ketley Quarry attracted ~100 objections. Community engagement early can reduce risk.
  • Previous refusals — Check planning history on council portal. Previous refusals set precedent — you need to materially address reasons.
  • Local plan conflict — Cannock adopted plan gives certainty; Dudley's plan still under examination (ambiguity until adopted).
  • Biodiversity / SLINC — Ketley Quarry had objections from Wildlife Trust re: Ketley Quarry SLINC. Ecology surveys now mandatory under BNG rules.
  • Tree preservation orders (TPOs) — Older residential areas frequently have TPOs. Arboricultural survey needed early.
