📍 Area Overview

WV7 covers the village of Albrighton in Shropshire, approximately 7 miles west of Wolverhampton and 8 miles east of Telford. It's a semi-rural commuter village with strong transport links via the M54 motorway (Junction 3, ~2.2 miles away) and Albrighton Railway Station (Wolverhampton–Shrewsbury line, ~1 train/hour).

The area sits within the M54 Growth Corridor — a strategic development zone with major housing and employment sites including Shifnal East (38 hectares) and the i54 business park. RAF Cosford (Defence College of Technical Training) and the RAF Museum Midlands are key local employers.

Albrighton has a traditional village character with a High Street (42 shops), GP surgery, dental practices, and two primary schools. It's popular with families and professionals commuting to Wolverhampton, Birmingham, and Telford.
