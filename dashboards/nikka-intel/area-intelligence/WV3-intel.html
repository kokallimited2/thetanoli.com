📍 Area Overview

WV3 covers the western and south-western suburbs of Wolverhampton, including Finchfield, Bradmore, Compton, Merridale, and part of Penn. It is roughly 1.5–2.5 miles from Wolverhampton city centre and about 14 miles northwest of Dudley via the A4039/A4123.

The area is predominantly residential with a mix of Victorian terraces, 1930s semi-detached homes, and modern developments. Finchfield has a village-like feel with local shops, while Bradmore and Compton offer larger family homes with good access to parks and green space.

Key employers nearby: i54 business park (Jaguar Land Rover, Moog, Collins Aerospace — ~12 min drive), Royal Wolverhampton NHS Trust (New Cross Hospital), University of Wolverhampton, Wolverhampton City Council.
