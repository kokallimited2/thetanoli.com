📍 Area Overview

B67 covers Bearwood and parts of Smethwick in the Sandwell borough of the West Midlands. It lies ~3 miles west of Birmingham city centre, directly west of B17 (Harborne) and east of B68 (Oldbury). Historically part of Staffordshire, Bearwood is now a vibrant, densely-populated suburb known for its Victorian terraces, diverse community, and strong local high street.

Smethwick is one of six towns in Sandwell and includes the wards of Abbey, Smethwick, Soho & Victoria, and St Pauls. The area covers approximately 14.73 km² with a population density of 4,345/km² — among the highest in Sandwell.

Key characteristics:

  • Young, diverse population — 76% from ethnic minority backgrounds (vs 27% England average)
  • Rapid population growth — +16% since 2011 (Sandwell +11%, England +6.6%)
  • Significant South Asian community — 43.5% Asian, 28.9% White
  • Below-average household income (£28,978 vs £37,863 national) — but Abbey ward outperforms
  • High private rented sector (28.4%) and significant social housing (22.9%)
  • 59% of LSOAs are among the 20% most deprived in England (IMD 2019)
  • Vibrant cultural scene — Bearwoodstock festival, Bearwood Shuffle, street festival

🚆 Transport Links

Bus: Bearwood has its own bus station at Hagley Road / Bearwood Road crossroads — the main bus hub in Smethwick. Key routes: 11A/11C (Birmingham Outer Circle), 80, 87, 89. Frequent services to Birmingham city centre (15-20 mins).

Rail:

  • Smethwick Rolfe Street (~2 miles) — West Midlands Railway to Birmingham New Street, Wolverhampton, Walsall
  • Smethwick Galton Bridge (~2 miles) — Links to Birmingham, Wolverhampton, Stourbridge, Worcester, and London Euston (via Avanti West Coast)
  • Langley Green — Local services to Birmingham and Kidderminster
  • Five Ways (~3 miles) — Cross-city line to Redditch/Bromsgrove and Lichfield
  • Sandwell & Dudley — Mainline station with intercity services

Road: Easy access to M5 J1 (West Bromwich), M6 J7 (Great Barr), and M42. The A456 (Hagley Road) runs through Bearwood into Birmingham city centre.

Metro: A West Midlands Metro extension along Hagley Road is proposed, which would bring a tram stop to Bearwood — a significant upside factor.

Airport: Birmingham Airport — 9.6 miles (~25 mins by car/train).

⭐ Overall: Good transport links. Frequent buses, multiple rail options, and motorway access. The area is well-connected despite not having its own train station within walking distance of all parts.

Strategy recommendation: Look for 2-3 bed Victorian terraces under £200k requiring cosmetic renovation.
