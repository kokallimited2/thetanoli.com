📍 Area Overview

WV9 covers the northwestern fringe of Wolverhampton, encompassing Pendeford, Fordhouses, Aldersley, and parts of Oxley. It sits at the border with South Staffordshire, with the villages of Codsall and Brewood just beyond. The area is characterised by a mix of interwar and post-war suburban housing (mostly semi-detached), modern developments, and green open spaces.

Major nearby landmarks include the i54 South Staffordshire Business Park (home to Jaguar Land Rover's engine plant, Moog, and Westcoast), Pendeford Business Park, and the Aldersley Leisure Village. The area is popular with families and professionals working at i54 or commuting to Birmingham.

  • Distance to Dudley: ~8 miles SE (12 min via A454)
  • Distance to Wolverhampton City Centre: ~3 miles SE
  • Distance to Birmingham: ~18 miles (25 min train from Wolverhampton)
  • Local Authority: City of Wolverhampton Council + South Staffordshire
  • Postcode Area: WV9 covers ~6 sq miles
  • Housing Stock: ~60% semi-detached, ~20% detached, ~14% terraced, ~4% flats
