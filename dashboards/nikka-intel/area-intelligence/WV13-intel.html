📍Area Overview

WV13 covers the historic market town of Willenhall, situated between Wolverhampton (3 miles NW) and Walsall (3 miles E), approximately 14 miles NW of Birmingham. The area falls under the Metropolitan Borough of Walsall and historically lies in Staffordshire.

Willenhall is traditionally known as the centre of the UK's lock-making industry ("Humpshire"). The town sits on the River Tame, bisected by the M6 motorway at Junction 10. The postcode covers the town centre and surrounding residential neighbourhoods including Short Heath, Stow Heath, and New Invention.
