📍 Location & Area Overview

Wednesfield is a historic market town located approximately 2 miles NE of Wolverhampton city centre and ~10 miles NW of Dudley. It sits on the northern fringe of the West Midlands conurbation.

Historically known for trap-making (mousetraps to mantraps) and coal mining, Wednesfield has transformed into a residential suburb with strong retail amenities at Bentley Bridge Retail Park. The Wyrley and Essington Canal runs through the town.

Ashmore Park and Wood End are the main residential estates. The town centre (Wednesfield Village) provides local shopping, while Bentley Bridge offers major retail, leisure (cinema, bowling), and a large Sainsbury's.
