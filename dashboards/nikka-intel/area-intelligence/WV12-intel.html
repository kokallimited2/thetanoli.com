📍 Area Overview

Willenhall is a historic market town in the Metropolitan Borough of Walsall, West Midlands, lying between Wolverhampton (3 miles NW) and Walsall (3 miles E). It sits on the River Tame and is part of the Black Country, historically famous for lock and key manufacturing. The WV12 postcode covers the Short Heath, New Invention, Beechdale, and Portobello neighbourhoods. The town has a large Sikh community (10.5%) and is ethnically diverse. A major catalyst is the new Willenhall railway station which opened on 19 March 2026 on the Walsall-Wolverhampton line, providing direct rail access for the first time since 1965.
