📍 Area Overview

WV8 covers the affluent western Wolverhampton suburbs of Codsall, Bilbrook, and surrounding villages including Oaken, Codsall Wood, and Lane Green. Located in South Staffordshire district, it borders the West Midlands conurbation.

  • Settlements: Codsall, Bilbrook, Codsall Wood, Oaken, Lane Green, Gunstone, Kiddemore Green
  • Council: South Staffordshire District Council (Staffordshire county)
  • Postal Town: Wolverhampton
  • Distance to Dudley: ~8 miles northwest (15-20 min drive via A454/A41)
  • Character: Leafy village suburbs with high proportion of detached & semi-detached homes, strong community feel, popular with families and commuters
  • Poverty Rate: 15% (below national average)

⚠️ Investment Note

WV8 is a premium suburban residential area with low crime, good schools, and high owner-occupation. It is excellent for family home buyers and long-term capital appreciation (boosted by major planned developments). However, it is not suitable for BTL or HMO investment due to low yields (2.7%) and limited rental demand. For buy-to-let in the Wolverhampton area, focus on WV10 (yield 6.8%) or WV11 (yield 5.6%). For BRRR, target older semi-detached properties in need of modernisation on larger plots.
