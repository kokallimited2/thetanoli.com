📍 Area Overview & Location

WS1 is the town centre postcode for Walsall, a historic market town in the West Midlands, approximately 10 miles northwest of Dudley, 9 miles northwest of Birmingham city centre, and 7 miles east of Wolverhampton. WS1 covers the town centre core along with the residential areas of Caldmore, Palfrey, The Chuckery, The Delves, and Highgate.

Walsall has a rich industrial heritage in leatherworking, saddlery, and chain-making, and is now undergoing major £140M+ regeneration focused on the town centre, including the Connected Gateway project, Saddlers Centre revamp, and Guildhall restoration. The SPARK Enterprise Hub near J10 of the M6 is creating 1,000+ new jobs in logistics, tech, and advanced manufacturing.

🗺️ WS1 sits at the centre of the Walsall borough, bounded by WS2 (south/west), WS3 (north), and WS5 (east). M6 Junction 10 is ~2 miles west; Walsall Railway Station is in the town centre.
