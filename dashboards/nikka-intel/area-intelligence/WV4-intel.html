📍 Area Overview

WV4 covers the south-western suburbs of Wolverhampton, centred on the historic village of Penn. It also includes Warstones, Merry Hill, and parts of Parkfields. The area is primarily residential, with large swathes of semi-detached and detached housing stock built mostly in the mid-to-late 20th century.


  • Location: ~2.5 miles south-west of Wolverhampton city centre; ~5 miles north-west of Dudley
  • County: West Midlands (historically Staffordshire)
  • Households: ~13,600
  • Postcode District: WV4 4xx (primary sector)
  • Character: Established suburbs with good green space — Penn Common, Warstones Park, Bantock Park nearby
  • Local Authority: City of Wolverhampton Council

🚆 Transport & Connectivity

  • Rail: Wolverhampton station (2.5 miles from Penn) — West Coast Main Line. Direct trains to Birmingham New Street (15-20 mins), London Euston (1hr 50min), Manchester (1hr 15min), and Edinburgh
  • Coseley Station: ~2 miles east — local services on the Wolverhampton-Birmingham line
  • Bus: Frequent National Express West Midlands services — routes 8, 25, 224, 530, X8 serve Penn Road and surrounding WV4 areas, connecting to Wolverhampton city centre (10-15 mins), Dudley, and Birmingham
  • Road: A449 (Penn Road) is the main arterial through WV4, connecting to Wolverhampton city centre and the M54 (J2 ~4 miles north). M6 (J10 ~6 miles east) via the A454
  • Air: Birmingham Airport — 25 miles east (~45 min by train/car)
  • Cycling: National Cycle Network Route 81 passes through the area

🏗️ Planning & Developments

  • Smithgate (City Centre West): Major regeneration of ~14 acres of brownfield land near Wolverhampton city centre. Approved — 1,000+ new homes, retail, cafes, restaurants. Phase 1: 330+ homes. This will boost the wider city economy and increase demand for suburban housing (people priced out of new city centre stock look to WV4)
  • Wolverhampton Investment Prospectus: City-wide regeneration strategy focusing on bringing people back to the city centre, improving public realm, and attracting investment
  • University of Wolverhampton expansion: Continued investment in Springfield and City Campuses drives student/academic staff demand for nearby housing
  • Transport improvements: HS2 (phase 2a — though currently under review) would connect Wolverhampton to London in ~1hr 10min; Midlands Rail Hub upgrades proposed to improve local connectivity
  • Penn area: No major planning applications currently flagged in WV4 specifically — the area is largely established residential. Small-scale infill development and loft/garage conversions are common
