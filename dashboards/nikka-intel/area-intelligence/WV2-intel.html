📍 Area Overview

WV2 is the All Saints, Blakenhall, Ettingshall and Graiseley area of Wolverhampton, approximately 1 mile south of the city centre. It lies about 3.5 miles northwest of Dudley town centre, and forms the southern gateway into Wolverhampton via the A449 (Penn Road) and A4123 (Birmingham Road) corridors.

The district covers 4.19 km² (1.62 sq mi) of largely urban residential and commercial land, with the major Dudley Road retail artery running through its heart. It is a densely populated inner-city area with a mix of Victorian terraces, 1930s semi-detached homes, and some newer infill developments.

Neighbouring postcode districts: WV1 (city centre, north), WV3 (west), WV4 (southwest), WV14 (southeast — Bilston).
