🚨 BREAKING: Commonhold & Leasehold Reform Bill — Draft Published 27 Jan 2026, Consultation Closed 24 Apr 2026

This is the most significant UK property legislation in decades. The draft Bill will ban new residential leases >21 years for flats and replace them with commonhold — where each flat owner owns the freehold to their unit outright, with shared ownership of common areas.

What this means for title splitting:

  • Traditional model (freehold + 999-year leasehold) will eventually be banned for newly-created flats — you would grant commonhold units instead
  • Current leases already created are unaffected — they remain valid, with new provisions for conversion to commonhold (threshold reduced from 100% to 50% leaseholder consent)
  • Transitional period not yet settled — the Bill is still in pre-legislative scrutiny, so the traditional model likely works for splits commencing in 2026
  • Freehold-to-leasehold splitting of existing blocks (where flats already physically exist on single title) should proceed under current rules before the ban takes effect
  • Action: Consult a solicitor on whether to use traditional leasehold or start adopting commonhold for new conversions
