🧠 Infrastructure Opportunity Map (New)

The biggest structural shift this session is the convergence of multiple mega-projects creating distributed worker accommodation demand across the UK:

  • HS2 (Birmingham/Solihull): ~£43.6Bn spent, Interchange Station in detailed design. Skilled tunnelling labour moving to finishing trades. Peak contractor demand shifting east to Arden Cross corridor.
  • Sizewell C (Suffolk): 2,000→8,000 workers. £12M Housing Grant Scheme. 20+ year construction horizon. Lowest competition for accommodation among all mega-projects.
  • Hinkley Point C (Somerset): 14,000 workers, 1,000-bed campus cancelled → more private rental demand. Campus cancellation is proof of model shift toward private sector housing.
  • AUKUS / Barrow (Cumbria): 20-year submarine programme. Ultra-low competition for accommodation. Remote location = captive demand.
  • Devonport (Plymouth): Naval dockyard regeneration. £multi-bn MoD contracts.

Key insight: These projects are not geographic substitutes — each has its own workforce, timeline, and accommodation supply. This is not a zero-sum game. A well-structured operator could build a distributed portfolio across 2-3 infrastructure hotspots.
