✅ Commercial Deal Checklist — Refreshed 31 May 2026

Pre-Purchase Due Diligence

  1. Use Class Check — E-class (prior approval via Class MA) or other (full planning required)?
  2. Article 4 Direction — Has the council restricted PD rights for Class MA in this area?
  3. VAT Position — VAT-elected? Opting to tax adds 20% to purchase cost. Structure accordingly.
  4. Business Rates — Rateable value, small business rates relief (<£15k RV), empty property rates (100% after 3 months).
  5. Lease Terms — Length remaining, break clauses, rent review frequency, FRI vs IR, tenant covenant strength.
  6. EPC Compliance — Minimum C from 2027 for commercial new lets. Check current rating and upgrade cost.
  7. Structural Survey — Floor loadings, ceiling heights >2.3m for resi conversion, natural light (window-to-floor ratio), fire safety.
  8. Contamination — Phase 1 environmental search (former industrial use is common in WM).
  9. Flood Risk — EA flood maps check. Insurance expensive for flood-prone commercial assets.
  10. Planning History — Extant PP, historic use restrictions, conservation area, listed building status.

Financial Viability Gates

  1. Net Initial Yield ≥ 7% (lower OK for prime covenants like Barclays, Mind, etc.)
  2. Rent cover > 125% of finance costs
  3. Conversion costs ≤ £1,500/sqm (WM average for Class MA)
  4. GDV uplift ≥ 30% for conversion plays
  5. Exit route clear: sale, refinance, or hold-forever
  6. SDLT: commercial rates (0-5%, no 3% surcharge) for mixed-use — significant saving vs residential BTL
  7. Stress test: +2% rates, -15% rent, 6-month void
