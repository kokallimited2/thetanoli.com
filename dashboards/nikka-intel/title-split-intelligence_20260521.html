✅ Title Split Pre-Purchase Checklist

📋 Due Diligence Phase

  • ☑ Freehold tenure confirmed — no leasehold restrictions against subdivision
  • ☑ Original GIA ≥120 sqm (where applicable for house conversion)
  • ☑ Existing conversion appears lawful (check planning history + LDC)
  • ☑ Units already physically self-contained with separate entrances
  • ☐ Commission Coal Authority CON29M search (DY/WV postcodes — HIGH risk)
  • ☐ Check for restrictive covenants against subdivision
  • ☐ Verify residential use class (C3) — not C4 HMO triggering Article 4
  • ☐ Commission full survey (RICS Level 2/3) for older properties
  • ☐ Check conservation area, listed building, Article 4 restrictions
  • ☐ Check EPC ratings — need C by 2028/30 for lettings

💰 Financial & Exit Phase

  • ☐ Run title split calculator — confirm GDV uplift ≥25%
  • ☐ Obtain pricing from Peppercorn Law (£995/unit fixed fee) or solicitor
  • ☐ Confirm bridging/development finance for 12-15 months

  • ☐ Model stress scenarios: GDV -10%, cost +20%, delay +6 months
  • ☐ Confirm lender appetite for newly-created leasehold titles

  • ☐ Check non-res SDLT eligibility for mixed-use purchases

  • ☐ Confirm exit strategy: sell, refinance, hold, or hybrid
