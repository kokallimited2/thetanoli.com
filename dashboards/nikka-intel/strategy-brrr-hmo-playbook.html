🎯 Target Parameters

  • Price: £80k-£150k (BRRR sweet spot)
  • Type: House only (exclude flats)
  • Beds: 2-4 beds
  • Areas: DY2 (Netherton), DY4 (Tipton), DY5 (Brierley Hill)
  • Status: Active (not STC/auction)

📊 Key Indicators in API Response

  • bmv_discount_percentage → Positive = below market value (BRRR)
  • estimated_rental_yield → 6%+ target for BRRR
  • chain_free → Faster completion, motivated seller
  • needs_modernisation → Value-add potential (refurb)
  • days_on_market → 90+ = motivated seller
  • price_qualifier = "reduced" → Price dropped

⚡ BRRR Strategy — Sub-£150k

  • Buy at £100k-£130k (distressed/dated terrace)
  • Refurb budget: £15k-£25k (cosmetic + essential)
  • ARV target: £150k-£190k (post-refurb value)
  • Refinance at 75% LTV: £112k-£142k
  • Equity left in: £8k-£20k (or ideally £0)
  • Target rent: £675-775/pcm = 6-7% yield

💡 R2R (Rent-to-Rent) Strategy

How R2R Works in Dudley

  • Find a 3-4 bed terrace: rent from landlord at £600-750/mo
  • Refurb if needed (furnish, redecorate): £2k-£5k
  • Let by room to 3-4 tenants: £325-400/room
  • Gross rent: £975-£1,600/mo
  • Your profit after rent: £275-£600/mo
  • No deposit needed (unlike buying)
