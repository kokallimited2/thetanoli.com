What This Actually Means

🏠 HMO Purchase + Conversion

If you BUY a house in DY and want to convert C3→C4 HMO:
Planning permission required. Article 4 removes PD rights. You need full planning application through Dudley Council.

Time: 8-12 weeks. Cost: £462+. Refusal risk: moderate-high depending on street concentration.

📋 R2R (Rent-to-Rent) — Still Works

R2R is NOT affected by Article 4. Here's why:
→ You rent the property from the landlord (no change of use)
→ You sublet rooms to tenants as a licensee/tenant
→ The use class (C3) stays the same — no planning trigger
→ Article 4 only removes PD rights for change of use

⚠️ But check: some landlords' mortgages or insurance may prohibit subletting.
