4️⃣ WMCA £200m Funding Pot — Application Process Detailed

FUNDING OPEN SCORE 7.5/10

Source: WMCA — Apply for £200m Funding

The WMCA's £200m grant pot is actively accepting applications. New detail obtained on the process and criteria:

StageDescription
1. Pre-application discussionSubmit scheme overview via WMCA form. Team assesses initial fit.
2. Expression of InterestIf pre-application endorsed, invited to submit formal EOI.
3. Full Funding ApplicationHPR team works with you to develop full application.

Key criteria for qualification:

  • Delivers brownfield remediation within WMCA area
  • Demonstrates market failure on the site (as defined by HM Treasury)
  • Unlocks new homes with minimum 20% affordable
  • "Funder of last resort" — scheme must not be viable without intervention
  • Target: 12,000+ homes unlocked, at least 2,400 affordable

WMCA Residential Investment Fund (RIF): Managed by Frontier Development Capital. Debt fund for residential developers:

  • Geographic focus: WMCA area only
  • Requires 20% affordable housing
  • For UK-based developers with proven track record
  • Useful as bridging alternative for Kokal brownfield sites

Takeaway: The WMCA funding process is now well-documented. For Kokal's brownfield sites, the pre-application discussion is the right entry point. Prepare a scheme overview document highlighting: (a) brownfield remediation need, (b) market failure (e.g., remediation costs too high for private viability), (c) minimum 20% affordable commitment. Kingswinford Stallings Lane (£1m WMCA grant, Keepmoat) is the direct precedent — demonstrate similar remediation need and community benefit.
