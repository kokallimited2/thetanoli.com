📊 Market Overview

The UK R2R/SA market remains in a bifurcated state in May 2026:

  • Standard lease-based R2R HMO: Consensus-dead. Renters' Rights Act (live since 1 May) abolished Section 21 and created periodic tenancies by default. One eviction or compliance event now wipes out a year's profit.
  • Leisure SA / Airbnb (B2C): Saturated. National occupancy ~37% in Feb 2026. FHL tax regime abolished. Platform fees 15.5–25%. Birmingham and London oversupply is acute.
  • B2B mid-term corporate lets (management partnerships): The only growing segment. £2.8B market, +34% YoY. Contractor housing, insurance decant, and corporate relocation are non-discretionary, recession-resistant demand streams.

Regulatory moat deepening: RRA 2026 enforcement is now live. LAs have a general enforcement duty. Fines up to £40,000 for serious non-compliance. This pushes corporate/insurance clients toward professional, compliant B2B operators and away from casual Airbnb hosts.
