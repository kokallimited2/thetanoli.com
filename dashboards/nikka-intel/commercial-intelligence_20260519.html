✅ Commercial Deal Checklist — Before You Buy

📋 Income Value (Yield Check)

  • ☐ Gross yield = annual rent ÷ price × 100
  • ☐ Net initial yield (NIY) after costs calculated
  • ☐ Yield vs area benchmark (see table above)
  • ☐ Rent review pattern (RPI/OMV/ fixed)
  • ☐ Void / vacancy risk assessed

🏗️ Redevelopment Value (Upside Check)

  • ☐ GDV calculated (residential or alternative use)
  • ☐ Conversion costs estimated per sqft
  • ☐ PD rights checked (Article 4 search)
  • ☐ Listed building / Conservation area status
  • ☐ Build cost + 15% contingency included

🛡️ Legal & Risk

  • ☐ Lease length and break clauses reviewed
  • ☐ Tenant covenant strength (D&B/credit check)
  • ☐ FRI / IRI / ERI repair liability
  • ☐ VAT elected? (option to tax check)
  • ☐ Business rates liability / empty rates
  • ☐ EPC compliance (min C by 2027)

💰 Finance & Exit

  • ☐ Finance type: comm'l mortgage vs bridging
  • ☐ Interest cover ratio > 125%
  • ☐ LTV 60-70% (commercial max)
  • ☐ SDLT: 0% <£150k / 2% £150-250k / 5% 250k+
  • ☐ Exit routes: sell, refinance, convert
