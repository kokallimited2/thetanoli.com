Netherton Arts Centre (£500k, Grade II listed) is currently on the market via Johnson Fellows. The cabinet is actively identifying further surplus assets. 6. Conservation Area pub conversions are possible but high-risk — two case studies in Brierley Hill.

The Plough Inn (locally listed, Brierley Hill Conservation Area) — retrospective PP sought for 4 one-bed flats already built without permission in 2020. The Red Lion (non-listed but "significant local value," same conservation area) — planning for 4 apartments submitted. Both show the council is pragmatically supporting residential conversion of derelict pubs even in conservation areas, as long as heritage features are retained. Score these as 6.0-6.5/10 — viable but require specialist heritage consultants.
