🎯 Today's Planning Insight — Grey Belt & Class MA Revolution

Key Learning: The December 2025 NPPF rewrite and the introduction of Grey Belt policy represent the single biggest structural change to UK land development since 1947. Grey Belt — land in the Green Belt that does not "strongly contribute" to its purposes — is now a legitimate development category. This is transformative for the West Midlands, where large swaths of Green Belt surround Birmingham, Walsall, and Dudley. Combined with the NEW +£234m Brownfield Housing Fund boost (March 2026) and relaxed Class MA commercial-to-residential rights (no floor space cap since March 2024), the opportunity window for land uplift plays in the West Midlands has never been wider.

Most actionable opportunity type today: Class MA Commercial-to-Residential conversions in Birmingham & Black Country town centres. Class MA now allows unlimited floor space conversion from Class E (retail, office, light industrial) to C3 residential — no vacancy requirement, no floor cap. Birmingham has high street vacancy — every empty shop unit in Dudley, West Bromwich, Walsall High Streets is a potential Class MA play. Prior approval (8 weeks) is the only gatekeeper. Conversion costs ~£50-80k per unit vs £150k+ for new build. This is the highest margin planning uplift play available in 2026.

Planning uplift multiplier: Agricultural £8,200/acre → residential with PP £200k-£330k/acre (Black Country). But Class MA commercial-to-residential: vacant shop bought for £50k-£100k → converted flat worth £120k-£180k = 2-3x uplift with NO full planning application, just 8-week prior approval.
