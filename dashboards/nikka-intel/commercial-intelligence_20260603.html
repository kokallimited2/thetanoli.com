🏛️ Commercial Property Finance — Rates and Structures (June 2026)

  • Commercial mortgage rates: 6.0% to 9.5% fixed (2026 range per Allica Bank). Variable: base rate + 2.0% to 5.5%.
  • LTV ranges: Typically 65-75% for investment commercial. Owner-occupied can achieve higher LTVs and lower rates.
