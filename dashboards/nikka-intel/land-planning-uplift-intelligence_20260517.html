⚠️ Planning Risk Checklist — Updated 17 May 2026

🔴 High Risk Factors

  • Class MA natural light failures are THE #1 risk for conversions. Mayfair Studio confirms inadequate natural light is the most common refusal reason. Deep-plan commercial buildings (former churches, large shops) are particularly vulnerable. Always commission daylight/sunlight assessment BEFORE committing to purchase. Budget £1k-£3k for this survey — it could save you £100k+ in wasted purchase costs.
  • HMO concentration risk in Dudley town centre remains. The 35-bed Regent House is a massive HMO for Dudley. If approved, it could saturate the local HMO market. But if refused, it signals that even town centre HMOs face headwinds. Monitor decision carefully — it's a bellwether for large-format HMOs in the Black Country.
  • Telford garden PIP could set precedent but also could be refused. The Spring Terrace application at The Rock is not yet decided. If refused, it creates a negative precedent for garden PIP across Telford. Don't commit to other Telford garden play until this decision is known.
  • Large-format Class MA (churches, supermarkets) needs specialist surveys. The Cannock Trinity Church conversion at 23 units will require full structural survey, contamination, flood risk, and daylight assessments. These costs (£10k-£25k total) need to be factored into the appraisal. The church's listed status or Article 4 status must also be checked — if the building is locally listed or in a conservation area, additional consent may be needed.

🟡 Medium Risk Factors

  • Canalside South construction timeline slippage. First phase completing Q2 2027. But regeneration timelines can slip. The "Canalside halo" effect on nearby property values depends on visible progress. If construction slows, the halo effect weakens. Monitor quarterly progress reports.
  • BHF grant competition. With additional £234m announced, more developers will compete for the same funds. BHF requires match funding and is competitive. Don't assume grant availability in financial models — treat it as a potential bonus.
  • Cannock Chase Local Plan transitional risk. Just adopted (March 2026). First 12 months of a new local plan can be volatile as policies are tested at appeal. Early applicants may face committee caution. However, the adopted plan provides more certainty than no plan.
  • Class MA's "reasonably necessary" restriction on external works. Class MA does NOT permit external building operations beyond what is "reasonably necessary" for the conversion. This means no extensions, no new storeys, no significant external alterations without separate planning permission. This constrains the GDV uplift achievable — you're working within the existing building envelope.
  • Biodiversity Net Gain on all developments. Since Jan 2024, all developments must deliver 10% BNG. For the 23-unit Cannock church, this means demonstrating measurable biodiversity improvement on-site or via off-site credits. Budget £3k-£8k for a BNG assessment and offset costs.
