📋 Commercial Deal Checklist

Check ItemWhy It Matters
✅ Use ClassCurrent class determines PD rights. Class E can convert to C3 under MA. A3/A5 (food) have different rules.
✅ Lease TermsFRI (Full Repairing & Insuring) = tenant pays all costs. Length, break clauses, rent review pattern critical.
✅ Tenant CovenantStrong tenant = lower yield but secure income. Weak tenant = higher yield but void risk.
✅ VAT ElectionIf VAT-elected, buyer pays 20% VAT on purchase. Budget for this. Recoverable if VAT-registered.
✅ Business RatesEmpty property relief: 3 months for most commercial, 6 for industrial. After that, 100% rates payable.
✅ EPC RequirementMinimum EPC C from 2027 for commercial lets. Currently E minimum. Check before buying.
✅ Article 4 DirectionCheck if council removed PD rights for MA conversions. Birmingham and some WM councils affected.
✅ Contamination RiskIndustrial land may be contaminated. Phase 1 survey essential. Remediation can cost £5-15k.
✅ SDLT CalculationCommercial/mixed-use: 0% £0-150k, 2% £150-250k, 5% £250k+. NO 3% surcharge. Huge saving.
✅ Finance ArrangedCommercial mortgages require 25-35% deposit. Bridging 0.75-1.25%/mo. Development finance 65-75% LTV.

📈 Strategy Analysis Deep-Dive

🛍️ Shop + Flat Above — The Kokal Sweet Spot

Why this fits Kokal Properties:

  • Low capital entry (£80-300k for WM high street units with accommodation)
  • SDLT saving: Mixed-use rates vs 3% surcharge on residential BTL
  • Dual income: Ground floor commercial + upper flat rent = two streams, one property
  • Class E flexibility: Ground floor can be retail, office, cafef, gym, nursery
  • Brierley Hill/Merry Hill edge: High footfall, affordable stock, regeneration zone
  • Exit options: Sell as investment, convert to flats (MA), or split title

Target areas: Brierley Hill DY5 > Stourbridge DY8 > Bromsgrove B61 > Halesowen B63 > Dudley DY1

🏗️ Class MA Conversion — Highest Upside Per Sq Ft

Key 2024-26 advantages confirmed:

  • 1,500 sqm cap removed — any size eligible
  • 3-month vacancy requirement gone
  • No affordable housing quota (saves 20-40%)
  • No Biodiversity Net Gain required
  • No CIL levy on conversions
  • EPC C minimum post-conversion

Best targets: Pre-war buildings with operable windows, narrow wings (60-70ft). 36 Tenby Street Birmingham (Grade II listed former commercial with PP for 3 apartments) is template.

Conversion costs: £1,000-£2,000/sqm. A 1,500 sq ft shop conversion to 1-2 flats at ~£100-150/sqft = £150k-225k total cost.

Biggest threat: Article 4 Directions. Check Dudley/Bromsgrove/Birmingham councils before committing.

⚠️ Risk Flags

  • HIGH Office market bifurcation: Grade A in demand; secondary offices declining post-WFH. Only buy with strong covenants or conversion potential.
  • MEDIUM Article 4 Directions expanding: More councils removing PD rights. Check before buying any conversion candidate.
  • MEDIUM EPC C requirement from 2027: Upgrading older stock can be £15-40k. Factor into refurb budget.
  • HIGH Void costs on vacant commercial: Full business rates after 3 months (6 for industrial). A 6-month void = £10k+ dead cost.
  • MEDIUM VAT on purchases: Many commercial properties are VAT-elected at 20%. Always check and budget for it.
  • MEDIUM Tenant covenant risk: Small independents can fail. Prefer personal guarantees or rent deposits.
  • HIGH Contamination on industrial land: Many Black Country sites have historical use. Phase 1 survey mandatory.
  • LOW Auction guide prices: Marketing tools, not valuations. Actual prices often exceed guide.
