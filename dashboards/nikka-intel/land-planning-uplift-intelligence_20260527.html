👑 King's Speech 2026 — Landmark Built Environment Legislation

SCORE: 8.5/10 POLICY STRUCTURAL

Date: 13 May 2026 | Source: Building Design

The King's Speech at the state opening of Parliament set out a major built-environment legislative agenda with six Bills directly affecting property and development:

1. Leasehold Reform Bill

  • Creates new commonhold legal framework — full freehold ownership for flats
  • Ground rents capped at £250/year, falling to peppercorn after 40 years
  • Ban on leasehold for new flats — commonhold becomes default tenure
  • "Beginning of the end for the feudal leasehold system" (Govt)

2. Social Housing Bill

  • Right-to-Buy eligibility extended to 10 years (from current 3 years)
  • All newly built social housing exempt from RTB for 35 years
  • Councils given greater confidence to build social housing

3. Small Business Protections (Late Payments) Bill

  • 60-day maximum payment term — most significant late payment legislation in 25+ years
  • BAN ON RETENTIONS in construction — targeted action on sector practice
  • 8% interest above BoE base rate for ALL late payments
  • Time limit for raising invoice disputes, board-level publication of payment data

4. Building Remediation Bill

  • Construction product manufacturers must pay for unsafe cladding remediation
  • New legal duty for building owners to remediate
  • Register of ALL medium-rise buildings (11m-18m) requiring remediation

5. Northern Powerhouse Rail Bill

  • Upgraded strategy — amends HS2 Crewe to Manchester Bill
  • Phase 1: Electrification east of Pennines (Liverpool→Leeds)
  • Phase 2: New route Manchester↔Liverpool
  • Phase 3: Enhanced Pennine links

6. EU Relations Bill

  • Cut trading barriers, reduce energy costs via electricity agreement, align UK carbon pricing with EU ETS

WM Impact: Leasehold ban on new flats will reshape all residential development — future schemes must use commonhold. Social housing RTB changes boost council appetite for building. Late payments reform + retention ban transforms construction finance. Northern Powerhouse Rail connects to WM via HS2 Crewe.

Scoring modifiers to add: Leasehold ban → +0.5 for future flatted schemes (commonhold simplifies investment) | Social housing RTB extension → +0.5 for council partnership JVs | Retention ban → +0.3 for construction cost certainty | Northern Powerhouse Rail → +0.5 for HS2 corridor halo
