🧠 Key Frameworks & Strategies

🎯 Auctions Are Liquidity Events — Auctions prioritise speed and certainty over bargains. Guide prices attract interest but are often set below true value. Reserve prices are the minimum acceptable by sellers.
🛡 Safe Deal Criteria for Beginners — Prioritise properties with clean titles, vacant possession, and simple refurbishment needs. Avoid complex tenancies. Ensure comparable sales are available before bidding.
🤝 Seller Motivation as Leverage — Target motivated sellers such as repossessions or probate sales for negotiation leverage.
⏱ 60-Second Deal Filter — Assess properties quickly by location, type, condition, and value-add potential before diving into legal packs.
🧘 Pre-Auction Strategy — Set and adhere to a maximum bid to avoid emotional bidding. Enter bidding late to gain strategic advantage.
📋 Post-Auction Process — Ensure readiness with solicitors and finance to complete efficiently. Contact lender and solicitor immediately after winning.

⚠️ Critical Warnings

🚩 Guide Price Trap — Guide prices are a marketing tactic, NOT an indicator of true market value. A 2-bed flat worth £200k might show a £75k guide price to attract bidders. Always base max bid on potential value + costs, not the guide price.
🚩 Emotional Bidding — The auction environment is designed to create pressure. Set your max bid before attending and stick to it. Enter bidding late to avoid being drawn into a bidding war.
🚩 Lack of Preparation — Failing to review legal packs or view properties before auction can lead to catastrophic mistakes. Use Docuise for quick summaries but always do your own due diligence.
🚩 Finance Delays — Ensure prompt communication with lenders after winning. Delays in funding can result in forfeiting your deposit.
