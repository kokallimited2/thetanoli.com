🆕 New Strategy Discovery: Post-Auction Distress Play

54 West Bromwich Street, Walsall is the first confirmed "unsold at auction, relisted above guide" pattern discovered. This creates a new strategy class: post-auction distress. When a property fails to sell at auction, the vendor faces:

  • Wasted auction fees (£2,700+)
  • Continued empty rates liability ticking
  • Psychological stress of failed sale + having to re-list
  • Testing the market higher = denial phase. After 4-6 weeks of no interest, price drops

Rule: Track Bond Wolfe auction catalogues for UNSOLD lots. Wait 4 weeks, then approach agent with an offer 10-15% below original guide price. For 54 West Bromwich St: target £170k, max £185k.
