📋 Per-Strategy Deep Analysis

Strategy 1: Small Industrial/Light Industrial BTL ⭐ TOP PICK

Why this wins in 2026: Small industrial units (2,000-8,000 sqft) in the West Midlands are in structural undersupply. The FRI (Full Repairing & Insuring) lease structure means virtually zero landlord costs. Tenant demand is driven by local tradespeople, builders, mechanics, and light manufacturers — recession-resistant. Yields of 7-10% are achievable on freehold purchases of £150k-£400k.

Example found today: Unit 15 Sovereign Works, Lower Gornal DY3 (£210k, £13,650 PA, 6.5% — reversionary upside to 8%+). Unit 1 Westminster Industrial Estate DY2 (£POA, £88k PA, strong yield). Dawson Brothers Timber Site, Dudley (£1.4M for 24k sqft + 2 acres — development play).

Strategy 2: Shop with Flat Above / Mixed-Use BTL

Best assets found today: Sparkhill cluster (916-922 Stratford Road) showing 9.1-9.8% gross yields. The key advantage is dual-income: ground floor retail/commercial rent + upper floor residential rent (or C3 conversion via Class MA). Finance is available as mixed-use commercial mortgages at 4-6% with 75% LTV. Business rates on retail portion only. Halesowen High Street lots at £99k showing 14%+ yields — high risk but extraordinary returns.

Strategy 3: Class MA Commercial-to-Residential Conversion

Key rule change (2025): Buildings no longer need to be vacant for 3 months immediately before application — occupied buildings now qualify. Maximum floorspace of 1,500 sqm. Prior approval required for transport, contamination, flood risk, and noise impacts. 78 Darlington St, Wolverhampton (offers invited, currently vacant) is a prime target — city centre location, Class E eligible, strong resi demand in WV1.

Strategy 4: Commercial BRRR

Most viable on: Small industrial units and mixed-use freeholds that are under-rented. The play: buy a vacant/under-rented commercial unit at 30-40% below rebuild cost, refurbish to Grade A spec, re-let at market rent, refinance at higher value. Example: A dilapidated light industrial unit bought for £150k, £50k refurb, £250k+ value post-refurb, refinance at 75% LTV = £187,500 — recovering most capital.

Strategy 5: Self Storage / Container Storage

UK self-storage sector now £1.2bn turnover. West Midlands specifically undersupplied at 0.94 sqft per capita vs 1.5 sqft in London. Disused industrial land/warehousing can be converted. Target locations: Tipton, Dudley, West Bromwich — large cheap industrial buildings with yard space. Planning for sui generis storage use is generally simpler than residential conversion.
