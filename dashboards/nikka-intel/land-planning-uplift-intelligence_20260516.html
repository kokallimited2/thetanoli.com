⚠️ Planning Risk Checklist — Updated 16 May 2026

🔴 High Risk Factors

  • HMO Over-supply — Smethwick/Bearwood corridor: Multiple HMO conversions approved by government inspectors against council wishes. The Smethwick 11-bed case shows the area is near saturation. Council resistance + inspector overrides = uncertain returns and potential over-concentration risk.
  • Green Belt still high-risk despite Grey Belt changes: Lower Penn is a TEST CASE, not a settled policy. Until the first Grey Belt application is approved (or refused and upheld on appeal), treating Green Belt edge land as developable is speculative. Use option agreements with delayed consideration to mitigate.
  • Backland development in already-dense areas: The £170k garden house demolition case (Walsall area) and Tennyson St HMO refusal show councils are policing garden developments closely. Professional planning consultant is non-negotiable.
  • Fire-damaged heritage buildings: Claughton Centre shows the opportunity but also the risk — structural stabilisation costs, unknown hidden damage, heritage consent complexity. Budget 20-30% contingency on build cost for heritage conversions.

🟡 Medium Risk Factors

  • Article 4 Directions on Class MA: Birmingham already has A4Ds. Walsall and Sandwell may follow. Check council websites for A4D maps BEFORE acquiring any Class E building for conversion.
  • BNG compliance: All developments need 10% biodiversity net gain from Jan 2024. For small sites (<10 units), off-site credit cost ~£5k-£15k depending on area. Factor into viability.
  • Space standards on Class MA: Minimum 37sqm for studio, 50sqm for 1-bed. Many old shop units in Black Country are narrow-fronted (4-5m wide, 15m deep = 60-75sqm) — may only yield 1 flat per shop. Check floorplate geometry before buying.
  • SDLT on mixed-use purchases: Buying a shop with residential above triggers different SDLT rates. Commercial SDLT rates are lower but rules differ for mixed-use. Get solicitor advice before exchange.
  • Ecological surveys on bowling greens/sports fields: The Wednesfield bowling green conversion may need ecology survey for slow worms, grass snakes, or reptiles common on amenity grassland. Budget £1k-£3k additional.
