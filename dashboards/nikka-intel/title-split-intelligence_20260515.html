🔍 Deep Dive: Commonhold Bill — What Title Splitters Need to Know NOW

QuestionAnswer
What's changing?New residential leases >21 years for flats will be banned. Commonhold replaces leasehold for new flats.
When?Bill published 27 Jan 2026. Consultation closed 24 Apr 2026. Pre-legislative scrutiny by HCLG Committee report expected Spring 2026. Unknown commencement date — but 2027 is realistic estimate.
Are existing leases affected?NO. Existing leases already created remain valid indefinitely. This is critical — title splits done before the ban are protected.
Can I still do freehold→leasehold title splits?YES, but act now. Traditional model works for splits commenced in 2026. Once ban takes effect, new flats must use commonhold.
What is commonhold?Each flat owner owns the freehold to their unit outright + shares in a commonhold association that owns common areas. More like US condominium.
Does commonhold work for title splitting?Unknown — but likely. You'd grant commonhold units instead of leases. Solicitor consultation essential.
