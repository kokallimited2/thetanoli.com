🚫 Refusal Patterns Learned

  • Small GIA (<120 sqm): Most common refusal reason. Two-up-two-down terraces fail NDSS. Do NOT buy for splitting.
  • Post-war ex-council houses: Low ceilings, small rooms, poor layouts. NDSS failure guaranteed.
  • Listed buildings or conservation areas: LBC doubles timeline and cost. Heritage architect budget required.
  • Properties in active HMO Article 4 areas: Not directly relevant to C3→C3 splits, but councils may conflate. Get LDC.
  • Deals where seller has already priced in split potential: Check for "potential" or "suitable for conversion" in listing — often means premium priced in.
  • Coal-affected sites without investigation budget: DY/WV = mandatory CON29M. Budget £5k-£20k+ for ground investigation if positive.
  • Owner-occupied residential mortgages restricting conversion: Most residential mortgages prohibit material change of use. Move to bridging.
