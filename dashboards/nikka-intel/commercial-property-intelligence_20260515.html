🎯 Strategy Deep Dive: "Retail-then-Resi Sequencing" — NEW STRATEGY

Concept

Buy yield, apply for planning
Secondary retail at 10-14% yield covers holding costs during 8-16 week Class MA prior approval process. Two exits: keep yield or convert.

Ideal Asset Profile

£80k-£250k retail
Vacant or sitting tenant, Class E, 500-2,000 sqft, no Article 4, good resi location. Target: Dudley Halesowen, Brierley Hill, Stourbridge.

Phase 1: Income Hold

10-14% yield
Buy with commercial mortgage (60-70% LTV). Tenant pays rates/insurance via FRI. Cashflow positive from day 1. EPC C+ needed from 2027.

Phase 2: Convert or Exit

15-30% profit
Once PA approved: refinance to dev facility, convert to 1-3 flats (8-12 months), sell/BTL at resi values. OR sell with PA as 'oven-ready' asset.

Why this beats pure conversion or pure BTL:

  • Pure conversion: dev finance at 0.8-1.2%/mo while no income = cash burn from day 1
  • Pure BTL: waiting for capital appreciation in a flat market
  • Sequencing: income covers all holding costs, the PA adds option value, and you can exit at any point
  • Lowest-risk commercial strategy for 2026: If resi market dips, just keep the 10-14% yield. If resi market strengthens, convert and cash out.
