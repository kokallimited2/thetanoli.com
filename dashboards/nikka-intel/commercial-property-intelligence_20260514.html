⚠️ Key Risks for West Midlands Class MA Plays:

  • Article 4 Directions: Birmingham City Council has active Article 4s targeting HMO/conversion hotspots. Check Dudley/Sandwell/Wolverhampton councils before committing.
  • Listed Buildings: Finch House (Wolverhampton St, Dudley) is listed — PD rights severely restricted.
  • EPC Trap: Min EPC C for commercial lettings from 2027. Conversion to resi resets EPC requirement but building regs compliance on conversion can be costly.
  • VAT: Check if property is VAT-elected (option to tax). This adds 20% to purchase price if you're not VAT-registered.
  • Building Regulations: Sound insulation, fire safety, means of escape — these are major costs in conversions, often £15k-£40k per flat.
