💡 Notable UK-Appplicable Takeaways

Submetering Utilities in HMOs strategy
Implement individual submeters for gas, electric, and water in HMO rooms to pass utility costs directly to tenants. This converts a variable cost (often the largest operational expense in HMOs) into a fixed-zero cost, improving net room yield by 10-15%. Many UK HMO landlords include bills — this insight suggests switching to submetered billing where possible to protect margins against rising energy prices.
Highest Rent = Highest Occupancy strategy
Counter-intuitive but verified: pricing rooms at the top of the market attracts tenants who pay reliably, stay longer, and cause fewer problems. The "race to the bottom" with lowest rents attracts churn-heavy tenants who damage property and create voids that wipe out any rent premium. Relevant for HMO room pricing and single-let BTL — don't undercut; price to attract quality.
Cap Rate vs. Interest Rate Rule formula
The cap rate (NOI / purchase price) must exceed the mortgage interest rate unless you're banking on significant capital appreciation. If cap rate < interest rate, the property is cashflow-negative from day one. Quick sanity check for any deal: divide annual net rent by price, compare to current mortgage rates.
"One Great Deal Per Year" Philosophy strategy
Focus on executing one excellent deal per year rather than chasing multiple mediocre ones. This aligns with Tanoli's preference for cautious, measured investing — patience and selectivity consistently outperform volume. Applies directly to the BRRR/Flip pipeline: one good £50k profit deal beats ten £5k deals with double the headache.
LTV Impact on Returns numbers
80% LTV = 14.59% return over 20 years versus lower LTVs yielding less. Higher leverage amplifies IRR but increases risk. The course advocates longer amortization periods to maximize cashflow — applicable to UK interest-only BTL mortgages where longer terms reduce monthly payments.
