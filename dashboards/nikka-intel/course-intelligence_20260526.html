40 Years Of Property Advice In 77 Minutes

Guest: John Howard · Host: Jack Smith · Channel: The Property Investing Club

John Howard — 4,500+ properties bought and sold across 87 UK locations over 40 years — reveals his core investing philosophy, current market strategies, and the principles that carried him through three property recessions. Covers everything from £50K flips to £26.5M tower block developments.

📋 Key Frameworks & Strategies

STRATEGY

1. Receivership Buying — The 2026 Play

Build costs up 30% in 3 years, interest rates doubled, prices down 10% in some areas = the "perfect storm." Developers can't build and sell profitably outside London. John has 5-6 of 8 current sites as receiver purchases — buying partially-finished developments from bankrupt developers.

STRATEGY

2. BRRR Workaround (3-2-1 Method)

Current rates mean you can't get all cash out on one refinance. Solution: buy 3, refurbish all 3, sell 2 (clears debt + delivers profit), refinance and keep the 3rd cash-free. "You may need two or three to get one out."

STRATEGY

3. Metered Utilities for HMOs

Old-school approach making a comeback: coin/prepayment meters for electricity and gas. Free-utility operators were "hit very hard" in the 2022-24 energy crisis. Metered = protected against cost spikes.

STRATEGY

4. Three Golden Rules (Distilled)

  1. Always maintain 3-5 potential deals simultaneously — never single-source
  2. Priority order: bank gets paid → you get capital back → profit
  3. Don't obsess over one deal — "If you're good enough to find one, you'll find another"
STRATEGY

5. Joint Venture Golden Rules

  • Take NO salary until the deal makes a profit
  • If a partner asks for monthly payments before profit — walk away immediately
  • Keep deal in company structure with a legal profit-share agreement
  • Personality balance needed: 'ballsy' deal-finder + cautious money person
STRATEGY

6. House-to-2-Flats GDV Uplift

Splitting a house into 2 flats lifts per-sqft value: £250/sqm → £300/sqm = 20% uplift. Plus council tax drops from Band D £5K-6K/year to £1K-1.2K per flat — significant hold strategy benefit.

🛠️ Tools & Resources

  • PiSA (Property Investors and Seminars Association) — self-regulation body for UK property educators. John Howard was a founder member. If an educator can't get PiSA membership, avoid them.
  • Refurb Property Meet — Manchester, Together offices, Tuesday afternoons. Physical meetup for property investors.
  • 99% LTV mortgages — Still available for owner-occupiers, despite the narrative that "no one can buy."

💡 Novel Insights

  • £100K abortive cost budget — Never seen this as a deliberate budget line. Most gurus show winning deals only; real veterans budget for deals that fail.
  • "Don't give up your job" at ANY scale — Even at 100+ properties, John advises keeping employment. Contrasts with "quit in 6 months" gurus.
  • "Are you buying?" question — Agents who ask this during a downturn annoy him. He's ALWAYS buying; the pace varies. Don't self-filter.
  • Metered utilities comeback — Post-2022 energy crisis, coin meters have become smart again for HMOs.
  • Over-promise trap — Tell investors 10% profit. If you make 12.5%, you're a hero. Say 12.5% and make 10%, you've failed.
  • Personality for JV success — Men over-promise (bankrupt), women under-price (buy nothing). Need both traits in a team.
