✅ Commercial Deal Checklist

✔️ Essential Checks

  • ✓ EPC rating (min C from 2027)
  • ✓ Article 4 direction check
  • ✓ Lease terms: FRI vs IR, break clauses
  • ✓ VAT election status (option to tax)
  • ✓ Rateable value + empty rates
  • ✓ Tenant covenant strength

⚠️ Risk Flags

  • ⚠️ Weak/unknown tenant covenant
  • ⚠️ Short lease under 5 years
  • ⚠️ Poor EPC (F/G) = cannot let
  • ⚠️ Ultra-high yield = check why
  • ⚠️ Lease expiry within 3 years
  • ⚠️ Listed bldg = restricted PD

📐 Key Metrics

  • • NIY = Rent ÷ Price × 100
  • • Reversionary Yield = MRV ÷ Price
  • • Rent psf = Annual Rent ÷ Area
  • • Cover ratio = Rent ÷ (Mortgage × 125%)
  • • GDV uplift = Resi GDV - All-in Costs
  • • Price psf = Price ÷ Floor Area
