📚 Strategy Library — West Midlands Commercial

💰 Commercial BTL (Retail)

Capital: £50k–£250k · Yield Target: 8–14%

Buy secondary retail (high street) at 8–14% gross yield. Let on FRI lease — tenant pays rates, insurance, repairs. Low management burden. Best in Halesowen, Walsall, Kidderminster. EPC compliance essential (min C from 2027).

Halesowen Walsall FRI lease EPC check

🏗️ Class E → C3 Conversion

Capital: £80k–£300k · GDV Uplift: 30–80%

Convert retail/office (Class E) to residential (C3) under Permitted Development. Prior Approval 8–16 weeks. Target 1–3 flats per unit. Check Article 4 directions! Best in Dudley, Brierley Hill, Wolverhampton where commercial/residential price gap is widest.

PD rights Article 4 Listed buildings

🏪 Shop-with-Flat (Mixed Use)

Capital: £150k–£400k · Yield: 7–11%

Buy building with shop (ground) + flat(s) (upper). Sell flat to recoup 60–80% of purchase cost, leaving commercial at heavily discounted basis. Alternatively, keep both for combined yield. Lower risk than pure conversion.

Dual income Split value Separate access

🏢 Development Sites (Full PP)

Capital: £200k–£1.5M · Timeline: 12–24 months

Buy cleared site with full planning permission. Known build volume, no conversion risk. Build new-build apartments/ houses. GDV known upfront. Best for developer-builders. Birmingham satellite towns (Aston, Perry Barr) and Walsall showing most activity.

🔴 Post-Auction Distress Play

Capital: £100k–£250k · Discount: 10–20% below guide

Track unsold auction lots (Bond Wolfe, SDL). Wait 4–6 weeks, then approach vendor below failed guide. Vendor has paid auction fees (£2,700+) and faces empty rates ticking. Target 12–15% yield post-offer.

Bond Wolfe Time-sensitive Income check

🏭 Industrial Multi-Let

Capital: £250k–£1M · Yield: 8–12%

Buy secondary industrial, split into smaller units. Each smaller unit commands higher £/sqft. Aggregated yield 15–25% above single-let yield. Best in Telford, Brierley Hill, Sandwell. Institutional demand driving prime yields tighter (5–5.25%), making secondary the sweet spot.

✅ Commercial Deal Checklist

✔️ Essential Checks

  • ✓ EPC rating (min C from 2027 for commercial)
  • ✓ Article 4 direction check (PD conversion blocker)
  • ✓ Lease terms: FRI vs IR, break clauses, rent review
  • ✓ VAT election status (option to tax)
  • ✓ Rateable value + empty rates liability
  • ✓ Tenant covenant strength

⚠️ Risk Flags

  • ⚠️ Listed building = severely restricted PD
  • ⚠️ Conservation area adds complexity
  • ⚠️ Weak tenant covenant = income risk
  • ⚠️ Short lease (< 5 years) = renewal risk
  • ⚠️ Poor EPC (F/G) = cannot let from 2023
  • ⚠️ Flood risk zones 2/3

📐 Key Metrics

  • • Net Initial Yield (NIY) = Rent ÷ Price × 100
  • • Reversionary Yield = MRV ÷ Price × 100
  • • Rent psf = Annual Rent ÷ Floor Area
  • • Price psf = Purchase Price ÷ Floor Area
  • • Cover ratio = Rent ÷ (Mortgage × 125%)
  • • GDV uplift = Resi GDV - All-in Costs

Improvement #1 — Data Source Fallback
Direct HTTP Fetching Restored

Both Tavily (HTTP 432) and Firecrawl (HTTP 402 Pay Required) were down. Direct requests with proper browser headers successfully fetched Rightmove Commercial pages (14/14 areas). This is now a validated fallback path that should be the primary scraping method going forward.

Improvement #2 — CloakBrowser Limitations Added
30s Timeout on Google Search Pages

CloakBrowser timed out navigating to Google search pages. The script uses 30s timeout which is insufficient for Google's load time through residential proxy. Recommend either increasing timeout to 60s or bypassing Google altogether and targeting Rightmove directly.

Improvement #3 — Property Detail Page Challenge
Most Property IDs Return 404/410

Individual Rightmove property detail pages for commercial listings often return 404 or 410. The search result pages (with __NEXT_DATA__) are the most reliable data source. All pricing, type, and tenure data can be extracted from the aggregated search results.

Improvement #4 — New Search Term Added
Rightmove Direct URL Pattern Discovered

Rightmove commercial search URLs follow pattern: https://www.rightmove.co.uk/commercial-property-for-sale/{POSTCODE}.html. Data extraction via __NEXT_DATA__ JSON script tag works reliably. Property detail URLs need #/?channel=COM_BUY suffix to display correctly.
