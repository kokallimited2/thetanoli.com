📊 Key Learning Points

🔑 1. Surveyors Value 5-15% Below Open Market ARV — This Is BRRR's #1 Killer

"Investors calculate ARV using open market comparables; mortgage surveyors value 5–15% lower using lending methodology." — Property Filter

Surveyors apply downward adjustments for: lending risk (not market value), comparable sale timing (4-6 month lag), rental coverage requirements at stress rates, and condition differences. The result: a £180k projected ARV gets valued at £165k → refinance at 75% LTV drops from £135k to £123.75k — £11,250 extra capital trapped.

Fix: Apply a Valuation Gap Penalty of 5-15% when modelling BRRR refinance. Always estimate refinance proceeds at 85-90% of ARV, not 100%.

🔑 2. The Money Left In Framework — Concrete Targets for Capital Recycling

A BRRR deal succeeds not on ROI alone, but on how much capital is recycled. Three thresholds:

Money Left In = Total All-In Cost − New Mortgage Proceeds
£0 (Ideal) → Full capital recycling. ROE is infinite.
<£10,000–£15,000 → Acceptable. Capital mostly recycled.
£20,000+ → CAPITAL TRAP. The BRRR has failed its core purpose.

ROCE = (Annual Cash Flow ÷ Money Left In) × 100
Target: Infinite (if £0 left in) or ≥ 15% if capital remains deployed.

Real example from Property Filter: A "working deal" at £140k purchase + £25k refurb + £7k fees = £174k all-in. Projected ARV £200k → 75% LTV = £150k. Money left in: £24k (trapped). After 15% refurb overrun (£28.75k) and surveyor valuation of £185k → mortgage £138.75k → Money left in: £39k. This deal traps £15k more than projected.

🔑 4. RICS ESG 4th Edition (April 2026) — Mandatory EPC C Gap Penalty

"Surveyors are now explicitly mandated to quantify ESG gaps. Assessors can no longer make favourable assumptions; if you fail to hit EPC C (2030 mandate, capped at £10k max spend), the surveyor directly discounts capital value by remediation cost." — Adam Lawrence

This formalises what was previously a soft risk. Key impacts:

  • Moderate roof issues → 5-8% valuation adjustment (not optional — mandated)
  • Structural damage → up to 20% value decimation
  • EPC below C → £10k max spend discount on ARV
  • No favourable assumptions allowed — thermal performance must be evidenced

Counter-strategy: Prepare a Valuation Pack for every BRRR refinance: complete schedule of works, building control completion certificates, structural warranties, hyper-local comparable sales aligned to RICS Red Book standards. This "completely removes the surveyor's ability to default to a conservative automated valuation model."

🔑 5. Subsidence Arbitrage — 30-50% Discount with Known Fix Path

Properties with active/historic subsidence trade at 30-50% below market value. Causes: clay soil shrinkage, deep-rooted trees (oak/willow), leaking drains washing away foundation strata.

Process: Specialist bridging → structural engineer → underpinning (£15k-£50k, 3-6 months) → 12-month monitoring period → 10-to-20-year transferable structural warranty → mainstream lending restored at 80-90% of open market valuation.

Arbitrage math: £200k market value property. Bought at £110k (45% discount). Remediation £35k + fees £10k = £155k total. After monitoring + warranty, restored value £170k (85% of market). Refi 75% LTV = £127.5k. Money left in: £27.5k. Monthly rent £900 → net annual CF ~£3,000 → ROCE ~11%. Not infinite, but significant equity in the deal.

🧠 Updated Deal Analysis Framework (Today's Synthesis)

Stage 1 — Strategy Definition

  • Before any number: State the strategy being analysed (BTL vs BRRR vs Flip). A property that works for BTL may fail for BRRR and vice versa.
  • Check strategy-market fit: 2-bed terraces <£100k → BRRR. 3-bed semi £100-150k → BRRR or BTL.

Stage 2 — Refinance Readiness Check (UPDATED)

  • 5-factor check: EPC ≥ C (30%), Compliance Certs (25%), Licensing/Article 4 (25%), Mortgageability (20%), Valuation Evidence Quality (NEW)
  • RICS ESG 4th Edition: Surveyors MUST quantify ESG gaps. Budget EPC C upgrade or apply £10k evidence gap penalty.
  • Valuation Pack strategy: Prepare RICS-compliant comps + works schedule + certs before surveyor visit to prevent conservative AVM default.
  • Basel 3.1 risk-weighting floors will tighten non-standard property lending further.

Stage 3 — True Market Value with Valuation Gap Penalty (NEW)

  • Find 3+ comparables sold <12 months, ¼ mile, similar type/size/condition
  • Apply Valuation Gap Penalty: For BRRR refinance calculations, use 85-90% of ARV (not 100%). Surveyors value 5-15% lower than open market.
  • Apply EPC C evidence gap penalty (~£10k) if not already compliant
  • Apply roof penalty (5-8%) and structural penalty (up to 20%) if applicable
  • Run GDV Stress Test: Target / Bear (-10%) / Stress (-15%). Deal must survive Bear.

Stage 4 — Full Cost Waterfall with Hidden Costs

  • Use the Property Filter detailed cost model: Management (12%), Maintenance Reserve (10%), Voids (8.33% = 1mo/yr), Insurance (£35/mo), Compliance (£25/mo amortised)
  • Critical: Model mortgage at stress test rate (5.5-7%), not product rate (4-5%). The Yield Compression Problem: rental income may pass at 75% LTV but fail at 80% LTV due to stress test rates.
  • Contingency: 15-20% of refurb (not 10%)
  • Cash buffer: 6 months holding costs + 20% refurb budget

Stage 5 — Money Left In Calculation (NEW)

Money Left In = (Purchase Price + SDLT + Legal + Survey + Broker + Refurb + Contingency + Refinance Fees) − (Adjusted ARV × LTV)
Targets: £0 (ideal) | <£15k (acceptable) | £20k+ (CAPITAL TRAP — deal failed its core purpose)
  • ROCE = (Annual Net Cash Flow ÷ Money Left In) × 100. Target ≥ 15% or infinite if £0 left in.
  • If Money Left In > £20k, flag as CAPITAL TRAP — refurb optimisation may fix, else reject.

Stage 6 — Three-Layer Profitability + Three-Scenario Modelling (UPDATED)

  • Show Gross Yield %, Net Yield %, Cash-on-Cash ROI %
  • Show detailed cost table (like the Property Filter example) to demonstrate real cash flow, not just mortgage-rent gap
  • Three-scenario mandatory:
    • Best Case: Refurb on budget, surveyor values at conservative ARV, immediate let at target rent, no unexpected costs
    • Base Case (Decision): 15% refurb contingency, surveyor 5-10% below market comps, 1mo void in year 1
    • Worst Case: 15% refurb overrun, surveyor 10-15% below ARV, 2mo void, 15% refurb contingency fully used
  • Mortgage Rate Sensitivity: Base (5%), Bull (-1.5%), Bear (+2%)

Stage 7 — Distress & Strategy Match

  • Subsidence properties: 30-50% discount, £15k-£50k fix, 3-6mo timeline → BRRR candidates requiring specialist bridging
  • "Requires modernisation": Genuine value-add signal
  • "Recently refurbished": Score Refurb at 0 — no value-add for BRRR/Flip
  • Amateur exodus creating consolidation: 84% of professional landlords expect yield growth (Magnate Assets, Apr 2026). Limited Co structures key.

New Mortgage Proceeds = (ARV × Valuation Gap Factor) × LTV Ratio
Valuation Gap Factor = 0.85-0.95 (surveyor vs open market gap of 5-15%)

If Money Left In = £0 → ROCE is infinite (full capital recycling)
If Money Left In > £0 → target ≥ 15%

Adjusted Refi Proceeds = (ARV × 0.85 to 0.95) × LTV

Where 0.95 = low risk (liquid market, consistent data, standard construction)
