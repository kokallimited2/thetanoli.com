🧠 Key Frameworks & Strategies

🔑 No-Renovation Flip Strategy

Buy at discount → resell without doing any work. The speaker bought a property for £125,000 and it was valued at £185,000 — a £60,000 paper gain with zero refurb spend. This is NOT the traditional flip model. The key is finding motivated sellers willing to accept below-market prices. Finance came from a graduate loan used as the deposit — creative use of accessible financial products.

🏛️ Receivership Sales — The Hidden Deal Source

LPA (Law of Property Act) receivers appointed by lenders sell properties at 15-30% below market value. The speaker bought a block of flats for £462,000 that was initially valued at £650,000 — a £188,000 gap. Six properties acquired through joint ventures. Receivership sales are under-exploited because most investors don't know where to find them.

🍻 Pub Networking — Your Best Deal Source

The speaker's first major property deal came through a connection made in a pub, not a portal. His thesis: informal settings are where real deals happen — people share problems (\"I need to sell\") before they ever list on Rightmove. Build relationships with people who move in property circles.
