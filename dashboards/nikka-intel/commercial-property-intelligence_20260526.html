✅ Commercial Deal Checklist

Pre-Purchase Due Diligence

  1. Use Class Check — Is it E-class (prior approval) or other (full planning)? Check recent changes.
  2. Article 4 Direction — Has the council restricted PD rights? Check HMO Research Database.
  3. VAT Position — Is the property VAT-elected? Opting to tax may add 20% to purchase cost.
  4. Business Rates — Current rateable value, small business relief, empty property rates (100% after 3 months).
  5. Lease Terms — Length, break clauses, rent review mechanism, FRI vs IR, tenant covenant strength.
  6. EPC Compliance — Minimum EPC C from 2027 for commercial lets (2030 for residential).
  7. Structural Survey — Floor loadings, ceiling heights (>2.3m for resi), natural light, fire safety.
  8. Contamination — Phase 1 environmental search (former industrial uses are common in WM).
  9. Flood Risk — Check EA flood maps. Flood insurance for commercial can be expensive.
  10. Planning History — Any extant PP, covenants, or historic use restrictions.

Financial Viability Gates

  1. Net Initial Yield ≥ 7% (lower for prime covenants)
  2. Rent cover > 125% of finance costs
  3. Conversion costs ≤ £1,500/sqm (WM average)
  4. GDV uplift ≥ 30% for conversion plays
  5. Exit route clear: sale, refinance, or hold
  6. SDLT: use commercial rates (0-5%, no 3% surcharge) for mixed-use
  7. Stress test: +2% rates, -15% rent, 6-month void
