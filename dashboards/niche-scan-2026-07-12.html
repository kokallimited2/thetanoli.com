⚠️ KDP Policy Watch (act accordingly)

Disclose AI Use

Tick "I used AI tools to create content" for any AI-generated text/images. AI-assisted (brainstorm, grammar, outline) needs no disclosure. Undisclosed AI → removal without warning, repeat = account termination.

Differentiation Bar

Duplicate / minimally-differentiated / mass-generated low-content books are being purged. Every SKU needs a distinct audience + benefit. Clinician-authored structure clears this easily.

Medical Disclaimers

Health logs/journals must carry clear "not medical advice / consult your clinician" disclaimers. Protects the credential and satisfies KDP content guidelines.

Quality > Volume

The "publish hundreds of long-tail books" era is over. Ship fewer, better, credibility-gated titles — the opposite of the strategy Amazon is now banning.
