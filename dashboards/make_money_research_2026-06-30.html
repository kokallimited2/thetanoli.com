The two combine well: park stablecoins for base yield while using genuine on-chain activity to qualify for high-EV airdrops.

Treat it as a portfolio sleeve, not a job. Base layer: stablecoins in audited yield wrappers for steady single-digit APY. Upside layer: small, real positions on protocols with credible un-launched tokens — actual swaps/LP/governance, not checklist farming (which is increasingly filtered out). Risk note: a 15% APY on a token that halves is a −35% position; size accordingly. UK: yield is taxable income and disposals are CGT events — log everything and use a crypto-aware accountant before deploying.

  1. Start small: a defined £-cap you'd accept losing, split base-yield vs airdrop sleeve.
  2. Use only audited protocols; avoid anything promising "too-good" double-digit stable APY.
  3. Track every transaction from day one for HMRC — this is the part people regret skipping.
