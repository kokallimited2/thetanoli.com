⚠️ Compliance is non-negotiable (June 2026 rules): Sourcing property for a client is estate-agency work under the Estate Agents Act 1979. To be legal you must: (1) register with HMRC for AML supervision, (2) join a redress scheme (Property Ombudsman or Property Redress Scheme), (3) register with the ICO, and (4) use a proper finder's-fee agreement with milestone-tied payments. Optional but credibility-boosting: NAPSA membership (25-point check, puts you in the top ~4% of compliant sourcers). Total setup ≈ £300–£800 + a day of admin.

Why it fits you: The bottleneck for most new sourcers is finding discounted stock at scale — you've automated that. Your competitive edge is a data pipeline others pay £28+/mo for and still work manually.

🇬🇧 UK Suitability Notes

✅ Regulation is a moat, not just a cost. The AML/redress/ICO barrier scares off casual competitors — once you're registered you're in a smaller, more credible pool. Budget ~£300–£800 and a day of admin.

✅ Be sceptical of "30%+ BMV" claims — genuine discounts are 15–25%; deeper usually means structural/legal problems. Always cross-check HM Land Registry (free, last 12 months) + Rightmove/Zoopla, and recommend a £300–£600 RICS valuation before exchange. Under-promise on discount, over-deliver on verification.

⚠️ Never take a client fee before you're compliant. #2 and #3 are safe to run immediately because they sell a tool / market data, not a sourced transaction; #1 waits for registrations.
