📈 Key Market Trends

🎯 The Subscription Barbell Effect

The £6.99/month utility app is a dead zone. Users aggressively cut recurring expenses. The barbell strategy:

Left Side
Free + ads/micro-transactions at volume
Right Side
Premium £30-99/month (B2B or specialist B2C)

🤖 AI: Revenue Premium + Churn Penalty

Apps with AI features generate 41% more revenue per payer but churn 30% faster. Users pay more upfront but leave quicker if value isn't sustained. The solution: proprietary data that improves with use (switching cost).

Cost warning: LLM-powered features are expensive. "The cost to serve a marginal subscriber is far from zero for AI products" (Phil Carter, Elemental Growth). Price accordingly.

📱 Foldable Opportunity

Foldable smartphones projected 30% YoY growth in 2026, accelerated by Apple's expected foldable iPhone. Foldable users spend £1,500+ on a device — £30/month subscription is trivial. Few apps optimise for foldables → first-mover advantage for high-value users.

🌍 Geography Matters

No "global app" exists. Regional dynamics dominate — plan for specifics, not blanket launches. North America install LTV is 2x global average. UK is a top willingness-to-pay market. Price differently by region (Germany charges 4.4x more than Turkey in Health & Fitness).

💰 Monetisation Strategy

✅ DO
  • Annual subscriptions for Health & Fitness
  • Monthly subscriptions for B2B productivity
  • Hard paywall (not freemium) — 5x better conversion
  • Web-to-app billing from day one (save 25%+)
  • Longer trials (17+ days convert 70% better)
  • Regional pricing (high-willingness: UK, DE, JP; lower: IN, TR)
❌ DON'T
  • Build a generic £6.99/month utility app
  • Build an AI wrapper (call API, show result)
  • Use freemium if you can do hard paywall instead
  • Default to weekly subscriptions for non-gaming
  • Ignore billing failures (31% of Play cancellations are involuntary)

🎯 First Steps to Validate

Step 1: Pick ONE niche from the three opportunities above. Don't spread thin — a solo Flutter dev should focus on one app for 90 days.
Step 2: Conduct 10-15 customer discovery calls. Talk to actual potential users (not friends/family). Ask: "What do you use now? What's the pain? What have you tried? What do you pay?"
Step 3: Build a landing page with a "Buy Now" / "Join Waitlist" button. Drive 500 visitors (Reddit, LinkedIn, niche communities). If <1% conversion, rethink the idea.
Step 4: Build a Figma prototype / Flutter MVP (2-4 weeks). Show it to the 15 discovery call participants. Get pre-commitments.
Step 5: Set up RevenueCat + Stripe billing from day one. Implement hard paywall (not freemium). Launch on iOS + Android + web (Flutter web).
Step 6: Post-launch focus: first 100 paying users. Use trial-to-paid optimisation (42.2% target for Health & Fitness). Build your AI data moat.
