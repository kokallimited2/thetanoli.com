Technical Analysis (41 videos, 94 questions)

📊 What the transcripts teach:

Trend Structure: A trend is confirmed with 2 higher highs + 2 higher lows (uptrend) or 2 lower highs + 2 lower lows (downtrend). Markets move in 4 phases: Accumulation → Uptrend → Distribution → Downtrend. Each impulse move has a corrective counter-move (profit booking).

Elliott Wave + Fib: Impulse waves 1-3-5, corrective ABC. The 61.8% Fibonacci retracement is the golden confluence zone for trend continuation entries. Volume spikes often mark new trend beginnings.

Break of Structure (BOS): When price breaks a trend line → BOS signals continuation. When price changes character entirely (CHoCH) → potential reversal. Combine both for high-confidence entries.

Key entry rules from transcripts: Trade from trend line zones, not single lines — treat as areas of value Use multi-timeframe analysis: higher TF for direction, lower TF for entry precision MACD crossover + volume confirmation = higher probability setup Place stops below the last swing low that would invalidate your thesis Technical + fundamental synergy beats either alone

Price Action (12 videos, 28 questions)

📈 What the transcripts teach:

Rayner Teo's framework (380K views) is the clearest: Price action is a methodology to identify imbalance of buying and selling pressure. Debunks 3 myths:

Myth 1: "Price action = support and resistance" — Wrong. S/R is one component, not the whole.
Myth 2: "Price action = candlestick patterns" — Wrong. Candles are entry triggers, not the strategy itself.
Myth 3: "Price action only works on daily/weekly charts" — Wrong. Works on ANY timeframe with sufficient liquidity.

The 3-Step Framework: Step 1: Identify current market condition (uptrend, downtrend, or range) Step 2: Find area of value to buy/sell (support, moving average, trend line, order block) Step 3: Wait for entry trigger (candlestick pattern at that area of value)
Stop Loss Rule: Place below the level that, if broken, invalidates your setup. NOT at an arbitrary % — the invalidation point of the setup itself.

Support becomes Resistance: The most powerful price action concept. A broken support level becomes resistance on the retest. Vice versa for resistance → support.
Q1 - Entry Question
From 'Price Action Trading For Beginners (Rayner Teo)': What is the recommended ENTRY signal?
Hint: Buy from area of value (support/MA/trend line) in an uptrend. Entry trigger = bullish reversal candle at that area.

Options Trading (8 videos, 20 questions)

📋 What the transcripts teach:

Options are leverage tools, not gambling instruments. The 4 key concepts from the transcripts:

Greeks: Delta (±1 to 0): directional exposure. 0.50 delta ≈ 50% chance of being ITM at expiry Theta: time decay — works AGAINST long options. OTM options lose value fastest in the final 30 days IV (Implied Volatility): high IV = expensive options. Buy when IV is low, sell when high Vega: sensitivity to IV changes. Earnings plays = high vega risk
Defined Risk Strategies: Credit spreads (bear call / bull put) for consistent income. Max loss = width of spread - credit received. No margin calls.

Position Sizing: Never risk more than 5% of account on any single options trade (higher than stocks due to defined risk nature of spreads).
Q1 - Risk Question
What POSITION SIZING rules does 'Options Trading For Beginners: Complete Guide with Examples' prescribe?
Hint: For defined-risk spreads: 3-5% of account per trade. For undefined risk (naked options): 1-2% max. Never size up after a loss.
Q2 - Exit Question
Where should STOP LOSS and TAKE PROFIT be placed for options?
Hint: Stop: 50% of premium paid for long options, or when the underlying moves against your breakeven. TP: 50-100% of max profit for spreads, 2:1 RR for long options.

Risk Management (6 videos, 16 questions)

🛡️ What the transcripts teach:

The single most important category. Every transcript agrees on these rules:

The Golden Rule: Risk 1-2% of your account on any single trade. This is not negotiable.

Position Size Formula: Position Size = (Account Balance × Risk%) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price) Example: £10k account risking 1% (£100) with a £2 stop = 50 shares
Daily & Weekly Limits: Daily loss limit: 3% of account — hit it and stop trading for the day Weekly loss limit: 6% of account — hit it and stop for the week Max drawdown: 20% — stop trading entirely, review your strategy, go back to paper trading
Never: average down a losing position, add to a losing position, or move your stop loss further away.

Swing Trading (4 videos)

🔄 What the transcripts teach:

Three high-probability swing patterns:

1. Daily Gap Up over Multi-Month Resistance: Stock gaps up overnight over a multi-month resistance level with a positive catalyst. Buy on first pullback after the gap. Ideal entry midday, not at open.

2. Long-Term Downtrend Break + 200 SMA Reclaim: Stock breaks above a long-term (months/years) downtrend line AND reclaims the 200 SMA with above-average volume. Entry at 200 SMA area, stop below recent support.

Timeframes: Daily for trend direction, 5-min for entry execution Indicators: 8 EMA (momentum), 200 SMA (long-term trend), volume (confirms breakouts) Positions: Days to weeks. Best entries found midday, not at market open
