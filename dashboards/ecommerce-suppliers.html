🎯 How to Find eBay UK Dropship Suppliers

  1. Platform route: Sign up for Avasam (£0 Free plan), AppScenic (free browse), or Hustle Got Real (£19/mo) — they have pre-vetted UK supplier catalogues
  2. Direct wholesale route: Search Google for "[niche] wholesale UK dropship" + contact suppliers directly with trade account application
  3. Trade shows: Top Drawer (London), Spring Fair (Birmingham), Bett (tech/education) — collect trade cards
  4. Company House check: Verify supplier's UK registration at find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk
  5. Test order: Always place 1 test order to verify shipping time, packaging, and returns process
💡 eBay compliance note: eBay UK allows dropshipping from wholesale suppliers. Buying from another retailer (Amazon→eBay) is banned and gets accounts suspended. Avasam, AppScenic, and AutoDS are all compliant wholesale platforms.

🎯 How to Find Amazon UK Suppliers & Wholesalers

  1. FBA wholesale route: Find branded products selling well on Amazon UK → contact the brand directly for wholesale/distributor accounts
  2. Online wholesale directories: TheWholesaler.co.uk, UKWholesalers.co.uk, ThomasNet (UK), Kompass UK — search by category
  3. White label / Private label: Alibaba.com → contact manufacturers → request samples → brand your own product (best margins, highest control)
  4. Amazon prep services: Companies like FBA Freight, James & James, ShipBob UK handle receiving, quality check, labeling, and sending to Amazon FBA
  5. SourceMango / SaleHoo: £50-70/yr directories of verified UK/global wholesalers
  6. Trade fairs: Autumn Fair (Birmingham), Pure London (fashion), ABC Awards (gifts/homeware)
💡 Amazon UK differs from eBay: Amazon has stricter brand gating, category approval, and VAT requirements. You need UK VAT registration for FBA (even if below £85K threshold for Chinese imports). Amazon also requires GTINs (EAN/UPC) — white label products need GS1 UK barcodes.
⚠️ IP Compliance: Amazon UK aggressively enforces trademarks and design rights. Always verify you have distribution rights in writing. Counterfeit claims = immediate account suspension + stock confiscation.
🔥 FBA Prep Service ended 1 July 2026 — now in effect: Amazon UK has discontinued FBA preparation and labelling services. Sellers must use third-party prep centres (James & James, FBA Freight, etc.) or prep inventory themselves. Factor £0.50–1.50/unit additional cost for third-party prep.
📏 Amazon product title cap (effective 27 July 2026 — TODAY): Amazon is capping most product titles at 75 characters (including spaces) and introducing a new searchable "Item Highlights" field of up to 125 characters. Media categories exempt. Existing listings must be adjusted. This significantly impacts keyword strategy — move specs to Item Highlights, keep titles lean for mobile. Source: sellercentral.amazon.com.
🧾 HMRC VAT consultation — UK marketplace sellers affected: HMRC is consulting (closes 18 August 2026) on extending deemed-supplier VAT rules to UK-established businesses selling on Amazon, eBay, and Etsy. If passed, marketplaces would collect and remit VAT directly, cutting seller payout by ~16.7% on standard-rated SKUs. Amazon has publicly backed the reform. Watch this space — it could reshape UK ecommerce unit economics.
