How to Read This Page

Portfolio Overview

Each portfolio started with £10,000 in paper cash. "Cash" is what is currently uninvested, "Positions Value" is the live worth of open holdings, and "P&L vs Start" shows whether the strategy is ahead or behind the £10k baseline.

Open Stock Signals

These are tickers the stock scanner currently rates as a BUY (strong signal) or WEAK BUY (weaker confirmation). "Score" is the scanner's confidence (higher = stronger). "Target" is the price the scanner expects to reach, "Stop Loss" is where the trade would be cut, and "Target Gain %" is the upside if the target is hit.

Recent Completed Trades

Trades that have already exited. "P&L %" is the percentage gain or loss after the position closed. Green is a win, red is a loss. "Days Held" tells you how long the trade was open.

Options Signals Today

Option contracts flagged by the options scanner today. 🟢 Call signals mean the model expects the stock to go up; 🔴 Put signals mean it expects the stock to go down. Counts show how many strikes/expiries were flagged for each side.

Trade Performance

Win Rate is the share of trades that closed positive. Profit Factor is total gains divided by total losses — anything above 1 means the strategy is net profitable. Expectancy is the average P&L per trade. Best/Worst show the standout outcomes.

Stocks Portfolio Value Chart

A daily view of the paper stocks portfolio total value (cash + positions). Flat sections mean cash was sitting idle; dips and rises show open positions being marked to market.
