7. How to read this page

  • Portfolio overview — the cash plus open-position value of two paper accounts: stocks and crypto. Both started at $10,000 on 2026-05-21.
  • Open stock signals — tickers the scanner currently rates as buys. "Buy" is a stronger signal than "Possible buy". The target is where the model expects price to head; the stop loss is where the trade would be cut.
  • Recent completed trades — signals that have already exited. Green is a winner, red is a loser. The percentage is the move from entry to exit price.
  • Options signals today — unusual options activity flagged on the latest scan, grouped by ticker. Bullish calls mean traders are paying up for upside; bearish puts mean traders are paying up for downside. A heavy lean one way can hint at where smart money is positioned.
  • Trade performance stats — quick health check on the scanner. Win rate is the share of trades that finished green. Profit factor compares total gains to total losses — anything above 2 is good. Expectancy is the average outcome per trade, so over many trades you would expect roughly this per signal.
  • Chart — the daily total value of the stocks paper account. A flat line means no positions were open (all cash).
  • Reminder — everything on this page is paper money. No real orders are placed.
