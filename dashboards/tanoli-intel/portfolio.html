❓ How to Read This Page

About This Dashboard

  • Portfolio overview — Shows total value of the £10k paper stock portfolio and £10k paper crypto portfolio. P&L is measured from the initial £10k starting point.
  • Open stock positions — Shows live paper trades currently open in the stock portfolio. Current prices from Yahoo Finance (1-min delayed).
  • Scanner signals — BUY/STRONG BUY/WEAK BUY signals generated by the stock scanner. These are recommendations, not actual paper trades. Score is 0-10 (higher = stronger conviction).
  • Completed trades — All paper portfolio trades that have been opened and closed.
  • Options flow signals — Unusual options activity detected by the scanner. "Call Buy" = bullish bet, "Put Buy" = bearish bet. These are market signals, not trades.
  • Trade performance — Analytics from the trade journal analyzer. Win rate = (% of trades that closed in profit). Profit factor = (total wins / total losses). Expectancy = average return per trade.
  • Portfolio chart — Shows the stock portfolio value over time based on daily snapshots. Each snapshot records cash + positions value at a point in time.
