📖 How to Read This Page

  • 🟢 Call Buy / Bullish Call — Someone bought CALL options expecting the stock to go UP. If you agree, consider buying the same call.
  • 🔴 Put Buy / Bearish Put — Someone bought PUT options expecting the stock to go DOWN. If you agree, consider buying the same put.
  • Stock — The ticker symbol of the stock seeing unusual options activity.
  • Strike — The price level they're betting on. Stock must cross this for the option to be profitable.
  • DTE — Days until expiry. 14-45 DTE = sweet spot for retail traders. < 7 DTE = theta decay accelerates fast.
  • Vol/OI — Today's volume vs open interest. 10x+ means very unusual activity. 50x+ = extreme.
  • Score — Combined volume/OI metric. 500+ = notable, 1000+ = very unusual.
  • ⭐ Rating — Trade score 0-100 based on 5 factors: signal strength (30pts), DTE sweet spot (20pts), strike proximity (20pts), liquidity (15pts), expected move reachability (15pts).
  • ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Elite (85+) — Perfect setup. High conviction trade. Strong signal + ideal DTE + near strike + liquid + reachable.
  • ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Strong (70-84) — Good setup. Most factors aligned. Worth considering.
  • ⭐⭐⭐ Good (55-69) — Decent opportunity but some factors are suboptimal (e.g., tight expiry or far strike).
  • ⭐⭐ Decent (40-54) — Interesting signal but trade conditions aren't ideal. Let it develop.
  • ⭐ Speculative (0-39) — Unusual activity exists but trade setup is weak. Watch only.
