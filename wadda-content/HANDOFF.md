## The Skill: `hermes-content-agent-workflow`

This was a daily cron job that ran at **06:00 UK time**, generating SEO articles from keyword briefs.

### How It Worked

1. **SEO Agent** (runs at 05:30) generates `content-briefs.json` with keyword research
2. **Content Agent** (runs at 06:00) reads the briefs and writes articles
3. Articles saved as `drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-{slug}.md`
4. HTML report generated to `reports/content-daily-YYYY-MM-DD.html`

### Key Data You Get

- **117 article drafts** across: VPNs, password managers, web hosting, cybersecurity news, SaaS tools, ecommerce
- **`affiliate-programs.json`** — all registered programs with commission details
- **`content-briefs.json`** — keyword research from SEO agent (search volume, funnel stage, suggested titles)
- **`keyword-database.json`** — full keyword research
- **2 templates** — best-of and comparison formats

### Pitfalls To Avoid

1. **DO NOT delegate article writing to subagents** — they consistently claim 2500 words but deliver 500-1400. Write articles directly with `write_file`.
2. **Verify word counts** with `wc -w` on the file — don't trust subagent claims.
3. **FTC disclosure** must be the first line of every article.
4. **Affiliate links** use `[AFFILIATE_LINK:name]` placeholder format. Replace before publishing.
5. **OpenRouter 402 errors** — if using GPT-4o via OpenRouter for article generation, you'll hit credit limits on long articles.
