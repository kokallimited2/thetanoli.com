## FAQ

### Do I need both Bitdefender and Malwarebytes?
Not strictly necessary, but recommended. Bitdefender catches 99.9% of threats on its own. Malwarebytes catches most of the 0.1% that slip through, particularly zero-day exploits.

### Is free antivirus enough in 2026?
Free antivirus (Windows Defender, Avast Free) covers basic signature-based threats but misses behavioral, exploit, and zero-day protection. In 2026's threat landscape, free AV is insufficient.

### Can antivirus stop ransomware?
Modern suites like Bitdefender and Norton include ransomware-specific protection with file rollback. They can restore files encrypted by ransomware if the attack is detected in time.

### Will antivirus slow down my gaming PC?
Bitdefender has been tested at 3-5% performance impact — negligible for gaming. Norton is heavier at 6-10%. Malwarebytes is the lightest at 1-3%.

### How often is antivirus updated?
Bitdefender updates every 1-6 hours. Norton updates in near real-time. Malwarebytes updates definitions multiple times daily. All three include behavioral detection that works without definition updates.
