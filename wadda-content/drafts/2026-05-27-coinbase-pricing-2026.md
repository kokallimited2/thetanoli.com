## The Problem: The Fee Structure Isn't Simple

Coinbase has multiple fee layers:

1. **Spread** — 0.5% mark on buy/sell prices (hidden in the price difference)

3. **Network fee** — Variable, depends on blockchain congestion
4. **Conversion fee** — 2% for non-crypto conversions (e.g., converting BTC to ETH)
5. **Withdrawal fee** — Varies by cryptocurrency and network
