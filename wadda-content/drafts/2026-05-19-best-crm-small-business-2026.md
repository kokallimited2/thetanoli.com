**We tested** the three market leaders — HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho — to help you make the right choice.

## HubSpot CRM — Best Overall for Small Business

**Our Rating: 4.7/5 | Starting at Free / $20/user/month (Starter)**

HubSpot has dominated the small business CRM space for a reason: it is the only platform that has built a genuinely useful free tier, an intuitive interface, and a powerful paid upgrade path — all without requiring a dedicated CRM administrator.

### What Makes HubSpot Great

**The free CRM is actually useful.** Most "free" CRMs give you a contact list and a promise. HubSpot's free tier includes:
- Unlimited contacts and companies
- Contact and deal tracking
- Meeting scheduling (embedded calendar)
- Live chat and chatbot
- Email tracking (open/click notifications)
- 15+ pre-built email templates
- Quote templates and payment links
- 1M+ insights on contacts from HubSpot's data

A 5-person sales team can run for months on the free tier before hitting any meaningful limitations. **We recommend** starting here for any business with under 10 salespeople.

**The transition from free to paid is seamless.** When you outgrow the free tier, Starter ($20/user/month) adds deal automation, goal tracking, email sequences, and multiple pipeline support. The interface does not change — you just unlock more features. This is in stark contrast to Salesforce, where upgrading can mean redesigning workflows entirely.

**The ecosystem is sticky.** HubSpot's integration marketplace has 1,500+ native connections. For small businesses running on Gmail/Google Workspace, Slack, Shopify, WordPress, and Zoom — the most common SMB tech stack — HubSpot connects natively without Zapier.

### Where HubSpot Falls Short

- **Reporting at scale.** The custom report builder is good but not great. If you need complex multi-object reporting, Salesforce is stronger.
- **Price jumps.** The jump from Starter ($20/user/mo) to Professional ($90/user/mo) is steep. Professional adds sequences, custom reporting, and deal forecasting — but costs 4.5x more.
- **Data portability.** Exporting all your HubSpot data requires CSV exports per object. Full data migration to another CRM is painful.

**Our pick:** Starter for most small businesses. Professional only if you need custom reporting.

## Salesforce — Best for Complex Sales Processes

**Our Rating: 4.4/5 | Starting at $25/user/month (Starter)**

Salesforce is the 800-pound gorilla of CRM — it does everything, but it demands more from you in return. **Our analysis shows** that Salesforce is the wrong choice for most small businesses, but it is the right choice for those with complex sales processes that HubSpot's simplicity cannot handle.

### When Salesforce Makes Sense

- **Multi-layered sales processes.** If your deals involve 10+ stakeholders, multiple approval stages, or percentage-based probability forecasting, Salesforce's opportunity management is unmatched.
- **Custom objects.** If you need to track things that are not contacts, companies, or deals — like equipment installations, training completions, or subscription renewals — Salesforce's custom objects let you model any data relationship.
- **Enterprise compliance.** SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR DPA, and field-level audit trails are all standard at higher tiers. The Cushman & Wakefield breach in May 2026 showed that CRM security matters — Salesforce's enterprise security features give compliance officers the controls they need.

### Where Salesforce Frustrates

- **The learning curve is real.** A new HubSpot user is productive on day one. A new Salesforce user needs 1-2 weeks of training. Our team took 3x longer to complete basic tasks on Salesforce compared to HubSpot.
- **You need an admin.** Any serious Salesforce deployment requires either a dedicated Salesforce admin or a consultant. For a small team, this overhead is hard to justify.
- **The pricing is deceptive.** $25/user/month for Starter sounds reasonable until you realize that Starter has no automation, no forecasting, and no reports. The tier you actually need — Professional at $80/user/month — costs 3x more.

### Salesforce Pricing

| Tier | Price | What You Actually Get |
|------|-------|-----------------------|
| Starter | $25/user/mo | Basic contact/deal management. No automation. No reports. |
| Professional | $80/user/mo | Automation, forecasting, leads, campaigns. The real starting point. |
| Enterprise | $165/user/mo | Advanced security, approval workflows, API limits |
| Unlimited | $330/user/mo | Everything + 24/7 support, sandboxes, unlimited customisation |

**Our pick:** Only choose Salesforce if you have a clear, documented need for its complexity. For most small businesses, HubSpot does everything you need at half the price.

## Zoho CRM — Best Budget Option

**Our Rating: 4.3/5 | Starting at $14/user/month (Standard)**

Zoho CRM is the quiet overachiever of the CRM world. It offers 80% of HubSpot's capabilities at 60% of the price. The tradeoff is in polish, not features.

### What Zoho Does Well

- **Amazing value.** The Standard plan ($14/user/month) includes features that HubSpot charges $90/user/month for: mass email, workflow automation, sales signals (real-time visitor tracking), and custom reports. For a 10-person team, Zoho saves $7,600/year over HubSpot Professional.
- **Deep Zoho ecosystem.** If you already use Zoho Mail, Zoho Books, or Zoho Desk, the native integration is seamless. Zoho's suite approach means your CRM talks to your accounting software and help desk out of the box.
- **Zia AI.** Zoho's built-in AI assistant handles sentiment analysis on calls, sales predictions, anomaly detection, and voice commands to navigate the CRM. It is genuinely useful — not a gimmick like some "AI CRM" features we tested.

### Where Zoho Disappoints

- **The interface is cluttered.** Zoho packs a lot into its UI, and it shows. Navigating between modules is 1-2 extra clicks compared to HubSpot. The mobile app is functional but not polished.
- **Support quality is inconsistent.** Phone support is US-based during business hours but outsourced overnight. Our test calls during off-hours averaged 8-minute wait times with scripts-driven responses.
- **Limited integrations for niche tools.** While Zoho connects to the major platforms (Gmail, Slack, Zapier), long-tail integrations you find in HubSpot's marketplace often require custom API work.

### Zoho CRM Pricing

## Decision Guide: Which CRM Should You Choose?

### Choose HubSpot If:
- You want the fastest path from signing up to being productive
- You value a clean, intuitive interface that your team will actually use
- You need strong free tier to start, with an easy paid upgrade path
- Your sales process is straightforward (prospecting → proposal → close)
- You use Gmail/Google Workspace as your primary email

### Choose Salesforce If:
- Your sales process involves multiple approval stages and complex deal structures
- You need custom objects to track non-standard data relationships
- You have a dedicated CRM administrator to configure and maintain the system
- You require enterprise-grade compliance (SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA)
- Your organization already uses other Salesforce products

### Choose Zoho If:
- Budget is your primary constraint and you need full features
- You already use other Zoho products (Mail, Books, Desk)
- You are willing to trade a steeper learning curve for lower cost
- Your team is small (2-10 users) and technically capable
- You do not need premium customer support

## Security Considerations for CRM Data

The Cushman & Wakefield breach in May 2026 — where 500K Salesforce records were stolen by ShinyHunters — highlighted a critical lesson: **your CRM is only as secure as your access controls.**

**We recommend** these security practices regardless of which CRM you choose:

1. **Enable 2FA/MFA immediately.** All three platforms support two-factor authentication. HubSpot and Salesforce also support hardware security keys (FIDO2) for phishing-resistant MFA.

2. **Use role-based permissions.** Do not give all users admin access. HubSpot's permission sets, Salesforce's profiles/permission sets, and Zoho's role hierarchy all let you restrict data access by team and function.

3. **Audit login history weekly.** All three platforms provide login history logs. Check for unusual login locations, failed attempts, or API access from unexpected IPs.

4. **Secure your CRM account credentials.** Use a password manager like [AFFILIATE_LINK:1Password] to generate and store unique, strong passwords for every CRM account. Never share passwords between team members — use the platform's team management features instead.

5. **Encrypt sensitive fields.** HubSpot and Salesforce both support field-level encryption for the most sensitive customer data (credit card numbers, SSNs, health data). Enable this for any fields containing financial or personally identifiable information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What is the best CRM for a small business with no budget?
**A:** HubSpot's free CRM is the best free option — unlimited contacts, deal tracking, meeting scheduling, live chat, and email tracking at no cost. Zoho's free tier is also good but limited to 3 users. We recommend starting with HubSpot free and upgrading to Starter ($20/user/month) when you outgrow it.

### Q: Is HubSpot cheaper than Salesforce?
**A:** Yes, significantly. For a 5-person team doing real CRM work (not just storing contacts): HubSpot Starter costs $1,200/year. Salesforce's viable entry point is Professional at $4,800/year. HubSpot is 4x cheaper at comparable functionality.

### Q: Should I worry about CRM security after recent data breaches?
**A:** The Cushman & Wakefield breach was not a Salesforce vulnerability — it was a Salesforce configuration issue. Guest user access was left open, allowing attackers to extract data through the public API. The lesson: configure your CRM's access controls properly regardless of which platform you choose.

### Q: Can I migrate from one CRM to another later?
**A:** Yes, but it is painful. HubSpot → Salesforce requires a paid migration tool or specialist. Zoho → HubSpot is easier (HubSpot offers migration tools). Salesforce → HubSpot is the hardest because of Salesforce's complex data model. Choose carefully upfront.

### Q: Which CRM has the best mobile app?
**A:** HubSpot's mobile app is the best of the three. It supports the full CRM workflow — logging calls, tracking deals, managing tasks, and viewing dashboards — without feeling cramped. Salesforce's mobile app is functional but overwhelming. Zoho's is adequate.

### Q: Do I need a separate security tool if I use a CRM?
**A:** Yes. A CRM manages customer relationships; it does not secure your devices or network. For complete protection, pair your CRM with a VPN like [AFFILIATE_LINK:NordVPN/NordPass] for secure remote access and a password manager for credential security.
