### Q: Does Norton 360 still auto-renew at full price?
**A:** Yes. Norton auto-renews at the standard (non-promotional) rate, which is typically 50-100% higher than the first year price. You can cancel within 60 days for a full refund.

### Q: Can I use Norton 360 VPN for streaming?
**A:** Yes. Norton 360 VPN works with Netflix, Disney+, and BBC iPlayer in most regions. Speeds average 180-220 Mbps on WireGuard — adequate for 4K streaming.

### Q: Is Norton 360 worth it compared to free antivirus?
**A:** If you only need basic virus protection, Windows Defender is adequate. Norton 360 adds value through VPN, backup, dark web monitoring, and identity theft protection — features no free solution offers.

### Q: Does Norton 360 work on Mac?
**A:** Yes. Norton 360 supports macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon). The Mac version includes antivirus, VPN, dark web monitoring, and cloud backup. Some features (firewall management) are Windows-only.

### Q: How does Norton 360 compare to Bitdefender in 2026?
**A:** Norton offers more features (VPN with unlimited data, identity monitoring) but at higher renewal prices and heavier system impact. Bitdefender has better proactive detection (98.8% vs 97.5%) and lighter resource usage.
