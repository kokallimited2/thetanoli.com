## FAQ

**Q: Do I really need all three?**
A: Yes. Think of them as three locks on the same door. A VPN protects your internet traffic. Antivirus protects your device. A password manager protects your accounts. They cover different threat vectors.

**Q: Can one tool replace all three?**
A: Some tools offer combined protection (NordVPN includes malware blocking; Norton includes a VPN), but dedicated tools are significantly more effective at their core function.

**Q: How often should I change my passwords?**
A: With a password manager generating unique, complex passwords, you don't need to change them regularly — unless Watchtower/breach monitoring alerts you that a service was compromised.

**Q: What about the recent BitLocker bypass — does antivirus help?**
A: The BitLocker bypass is a hardware-level attack. Antivirus won't prevent it. Use a VPN for data-in-transit protection and set a BitLocker PIN.

**Q: Is free antivirus enough?**
A: Free antivirus (like Windows Defender) is better than nothing. But paid tools like [AFFILIATE_LINK:Bitdefender] offer significantly better zero-day protection and additional features like VPN, password manager, and ransomware remediation.

**Q: I use ChatGPT — should I be worried about the OpenAI lawsuit?**
A: Yes. Use a VPN and a password manager to protect your AI usage. Read our [OpenAI privacy lawsuit guide](INTERNAL_LINK:reactive_OpenAI) for full details.
