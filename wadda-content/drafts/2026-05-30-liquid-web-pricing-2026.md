## Hidden Costs & What's NOT Included

Liquid Web is more transparent than most, but watch for these:

- **Domain registration**: Not included — you'll need to configure your own domain or buy separately
- **CDN overages**: Cloudflare CDN is included, but excessive bandwidth may incur additional charges
- **Additional site migrations**: First migration is free; additional ones cost $50-$150 depending on complexity
- **Dedicated IP**: Included on VPS and Dedicated plans, but Nexcess plans use shared IPs by default
- **Off-site backups beyond 30 days**: If you want backups older than 30 days, you need to arrange off-site backup yourself
- **SSL certificate for WooCommerce**: Includes AutoSSL but Premium SSL certificates (EV/OV) are extra
- **Phone support**: Available for all plans, but wait times during peak hours can exceed claimed SLA
