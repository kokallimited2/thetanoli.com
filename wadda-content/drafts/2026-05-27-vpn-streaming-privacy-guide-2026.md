## FAQ

### Will a VPN slow down my streaming?
Yes, by 10-20% on nearby servers and 30-50% on distant servers. NordVPN's NordLynx protocol minimizes this — 4K streaming works on nearby servers.

### Is it legal to use a VPN for streaming?
Using a VPN is legal in most countries. However, bypassing geo-restrictions may violate your streaming service's terms of service.

### Can I use a free VPN for streaming?
Free VPNs are blocked by most streaming services, have slow speeds, and often log and sell your data. Paid VPNs start at $3.99/month — worth it.

### Which VPN works best with Netflix?
NordVPN consistently unblocks Netflix US, UK, Canada, Japan, and Australia. ExpressVPN is a close second.

### Do I need a VPN for privacy if I'm not streaming?
Yes. Your ISP tracks everything you do online and can sell that data. A VPN encrypts all your traffic, not just streaming.
