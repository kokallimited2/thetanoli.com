## FAQ

### Is Shopify really $1/month?
For the first 3 months on the Basic plan, yes. Then it's $29/month for Basic.

### Can I use a different payment processor?
Yes, but Shopify charges a 0.5%-2.0% additional fee for third-party gateways. Using Shopify Payments avoids this entirely.

### How many apps do I really need?
For a basic store: 3-5 apps (SEO, reviews, email marketing, analytics). For a fully-featured store: 8-12 apps.

### Does Shopify include hosting?
Yes. All Shopify plans include unlimited, secure hosting with a free SSL certificate and CDN.

### Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Your store stays exactly as-is — only features change.

### Are there any fees I can't avoid?
Transaction fees are unavoidable (they're the cost of accepting payments). App costs depend on your needs. Use free apps where possible and review your app subscriptions quarterly.
