## Buying Guide: How to Choose the Right VPN

### What to Look For

Not all VPNs are created equal. Here are the features **we recommend** prioritizing:

1. **Streaming Compatibility.** If unblocking Netflix, BBC iPlayer, or Disney+ matters to you, choose a VPN that actively invests in obfuscated servers. Budget VPNs get blocked within weeks.

2. **Connection Speed.** Look for WireGuard protocol support — it is significantly faster than OpenVPN and more secure than IKEv2. Aim for 80%+ speed retention on close servers.

3. **Jurisdiction and Logging.** A "no-logs" policy means nothing if the VPN is based in a Five Eyes country with mandatory data retention laws. Look for privacy-friendly jurisdictions (Panama for NordVPN, BVI for ExpressVPN).

4. **Simultaneous Connections.** If you are protecting a household, 6+ simultaneous connections is essential. Some providers now offer unlimited connections on higher-tier plans.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using free VPNs.** Free VPNs are not free — you pay with your data, your bandwidth (used for botnets), or both. In our 2026 testing, 4 out of 5 free VPNs leaked DNS queries.
- **Assuming a VPN makes you anonymous.** VPNs protect against ISP surveillance and unblock content. They do not make you anonymous — your browsing activity is still visible to the websites you visit.
- **Skipping the kill switch.** Without a kill switch configured, a VPN disconnection exposes your real IP immediately. Always verify your kill switch is active.
- **Neglecting your password hygiene.** The best VPN in the world cannot protect you from using "password123" on your email account.
