Here's a statistic that should make every business owner sit up: **over 60% of small businesses that suffer a cyberattack shut down within six months.** And the most common entry point? Unsecured remote access.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use a consumer VPN for my business?**
A: Technically yes, but you shouldn't. Consumer VPNs lack team management, dedicated IPs, and audit logs. If an employee leaves, you can't revoke their access. If there's a security incident, you have no logs.

**Q: How many employees do I need before considering a business VPN?**
A: If you have even one remote employee who accesses company systems, you need a business VPN. Size doesn't matter — security does.

**Q: What's the difference between NordLayer and NordVPN?**
A: NordVPN is a consumer VPN. NordLayer is a business security platform with zero-trust architecture, team management, and compliance features. They share the same parent company but are entirely different products.

**Q: Does a business VPN slow down the internet?**
A: Some slowdown is inevitable due to encryption overhead. In our tests, NordLayer maintained 85-92% of base speed — barely noticeable for most business applications.

**Q: Can I switch providers easily?**
A: Most providers allow mid-contract cancellation or have month-to-month options. We recommend starting with a free trial to test before committing.

**Q: What about the recent cPanel vulnerability — does a VPN help?**
A: A business VPN encrypts traffic between your team and your servers, but it doesn't patch server-side vulnerabilities. If you run cPanel, make sure to [patch CVE-2026-41940 immediately](INTERNAL_LINK:reactive_cPanel). A VPN adds defense-in-depth, but patching is the priority.
