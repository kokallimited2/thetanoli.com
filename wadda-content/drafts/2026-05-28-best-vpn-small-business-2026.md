## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do I really need a business VPN, or can I use a consumer VPN?

**A:** If you have 1-3 employees and don't need centralized management, a consumer VPN works fine. For 3+ employees, a business VPN's team management dashboard, centralized billing, and policy enforcement features save hours of IT admin time.

### Q: Will a business VPN slow down my internet?

**A:** Our tests showed 10-25% speed reduction on business VPNs vs 5-15% on consumer VPNs. The extra overhead comes from traffic inspection and policy enforcement. NordLayer's NordLynx protocol was the fastest among business VPNs.

### Q: Can I use a business VPN for personal use too?

**A:** Yes — most business VPNs allow personal traffic. NordLayer and Perimeter 81 don't restrict personal browsing. PureVPN's unlimited server access works for personal use.

### Q: Do all business VPNs offer dedicated IPs?

**A:** NordLayer, Perimeter 81, and PureVPN all offer dedicated IPs for business accounts. NordVPN does not offer business-specific dedicated IPs.

### Q: What about ExpressVPN for business?

**A:** ExpressVPN offers a business plan called ExpressVPN Business that includes team management features price-matched to their consumer tier. Good option if you want premium, but the team features are less mature than NordLayer.

### Q: How do I get started?

**A:** All four providers offer free trials. Start with NordLayer — set up a 7-day trial, invite one or two team members, and see how the management dashboard works.
