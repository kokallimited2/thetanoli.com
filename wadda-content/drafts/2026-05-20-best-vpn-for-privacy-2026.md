## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is a VPN necessary in 2026 with all the current privacy laws?

**A:** Yes. While GDPR and similar laws provide some protection, they don't stop ISPs from collecting data about your browsing habits, advertisers from tracking you, or attackers from intercepting your traffic on public WiFi. A VPN adds an essential encryption layer that no law provides.

### Q: Which VPN is best for torrenting?

**A:** PureVPN offers 138+ dedicated P2P servers and delivered the fastest torrent speeds in our testing. NordVPN also supports P2P on all servers and offers SOCKS5 proxy support. ExpressVPN allows P2P but routes through their standard servers.

### Q: Can I use a VPN on my router?

**A:** Yes — all three providers offer router VPN configuration guides. NordVPN has the simplest setup with automatic configuration files for 20+ router models. Router-level VPN protects every device on your network, including smart home devices.

### Q: How do I know a VPN is truly no-log?
